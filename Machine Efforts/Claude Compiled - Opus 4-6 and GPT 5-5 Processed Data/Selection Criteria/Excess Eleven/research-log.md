## WHAT WE DON'T KNOW (GAPS)

### Medical/Physical Testing Specifics
- Exact tests administered at Brooks for Group 6 (we have Don Lind's Group 5 account as proxy: Rorschach, encephalograms, treadmill, centrifuge, hot water immersion, cold water in ears)
- Whether the "torture chamber thoroughness" tests O'Leary describes in his book match the Group 5 protocol or differed
- Whether the medical standards were formally identical to Group 4 (Spaceline says "qualifications were identical to Group 4" but this may refer only to educational requirements)
- What specific physical exam criteria were used (vision, hearing, cardiovascular benchmarks)

### Psychological Testing Specifics
- Exact psychological instruments used (MMPI? Rorschach confirmed for Group 5; unknown if same battery for Group 6)
- What O'Leary means by "a relatively smart person trying to figure out what the psychologist wants to hear"
- Full context of Slayton's "slipped through the gaps of psychological testing" comment about O'Leary

### NAS Screening Criteria
- Exact criteria used by NAS to narrow 923 to 69 (we know "academic credentials" but not specifics)
- Whether the NAS ad hoc committee composition for the 1967 round is documented
- Whether NAS ranked candidates or simply passed/failed them

### Interview Process
- What questions were asked during MSC interviews
- How the T-38 ride was evaluated (pass/fail? subjective assessment? medical monitoring?)
- Whether there were additional tests at MSC beyond interviews and T-38 ride

## DISCREPANCIES NOTED

1. **Announcement date:** Wikipedia and Grokipedia say August 11, 1967. NASA.gov (John Uri), NSS, and NASA's *Astronautics and Aeronautics, 1967* chronology / NASA Release 67-211 support August 4, 1967. Dataset now uses August 4, 1967 for `Date of Selection`.

2. **Age limit:** Wireworld says "under 35." Wikipedia says "Born on or after August 1, 1930" (would make max age ~37 at selection). Spaceline says "not older than 40" for this group. Multiple sources say age limit was "abolished" or "relaxed" for Group 6, allowing Henize (born 1926, age 41) and Thornton (born 1929, age 38).

3. **Number of applicants:** All sources consistently say 923.

4. **"All civilians" claim:** National Academies source says "all of whom were civilians." This is technically true at time of selection -- Musgrave had prior USMC enlisted service but was civilian by 1967. However, this is worth noting for the aging anomaly project since military vs. civilian backgrounds are a key variable.

## FUTURE LEADS

1. **TOP PRIORITY: Obtain O'Leary's "The Making of an Ex-Astronaut" (1970).** This is the single richest source for firsthand Group 6 selection detail. Out of print but available used. Google Books limited preview may have some pages.

2. **Access SP-4214 Ch. 5-10 directly** (https://www.hq.nasa.gov/pao/History/SP-4214/ch5-10.html) for the official NASA historical account of the second scientist-astronaut selection.

3. **Read Joseph Allen's full oral history** at JSC History Portal for Group 6 selection details from inside the process.

4. **Read Wilmot Hess oral history** -- he was on the interview panel and was NASA's Director of Science and Applications. His perspective on selection criteria would be valuable.

5. **Check NASA NTRS** (ntrs.nasa.gov) for the original 1967 press release and any formal selection criteria documents.

6. **NSS articles need full-page access:** "In Excess: The Travails of 1967's Astronaut Class" and "Infamous Spaceflight Controversies" both contain substantial detail that was only partially captured via search snippets.

7. **Slayton & Cassutt "Deke!" (1994)** for Slayton's perspective on Group 6 selection, particularly the O'Leary psychological testing comment.

8. **NSS "First Aussie" Philip Chapman series (Parts 1-3)** for Chapman's firsthand account of selection as a naturalized citizen.

9. **Trames journal paper "Astronaut Selection and Potential Risk"** (2024, https://kirj.ee/wp-content/plugins/kirj/pub/Trames-3-2024-211-236_20240825124645.pdf) -- academic analysis of astronaut selection criteria evolution.
