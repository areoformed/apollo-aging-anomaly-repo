## Key Findings So Far

### What the Medical Examination at Brooks AFB Included (Group 3, 1963)
Based on compiled sources (Wikipedia Group 3, Group 5, Lovelace documentation, National Space Academy):

**Cardiovascular:**
- Cardiograms (ECG/EKG)
- Treadmill stress test
- Harvard Step test
- Bicycle ergometer (cardiovascular fitness)
- Cold Pressor test (ice water immersion — administered as surprise)
- Tilt table testing

**Neurological:**
- Electroencephalograms (EEG)
- Ice water caloric test (cold water poured into one ear to test vestibular response)
- Motion sickness testing (spun in darkened rooms)
- Hypoxia tests (reaction to oxygen loss)

**Psychological/Psychiatric:**
- Rorschach inkblot tests
- 13 psychological tests examining motivation and personality
- Dozens of aptitude tests
- "Who are you?" — 20 self-definitions required
- Blank white paper shown — asked to describe what they saw
- Psychiatrist interview
- Isolation protocols

**Physical/Metabolic:**
- Extensive X-rays
- Blood sugar tests
- Detailed eye examinations
- Ear, nose, throat analysis
- Radiation level testing
- Total body water tests
- Specific gravity tests
- General physical by three separate examiners (flight surgeon, internist, psychiatrist)

**Stress/Environmental:**
- Centrifuge (g-force endurance)
- Pressure suit familiarization
- Hot water immersion (hand plunged into hot water)

### What Changed for Group 3 (vs. Groups 1-2)
1. **Test pilot requirement dropped** — 1,000 hours jet time accepted as substitute (first time)
2. **Age limit lowered** — from 35 to 34 (by June 30, 1963)
3. **Education expanded** — biological sciences now accepted alongside engineering and physical sciences
4. **Medical eval location stabilized** — Brooks AFB (same as Group 2; Mercury used Lovelace + Wright-Patterson)
5. **Slayton's points system** — formalized scoring: 10 pts academics, 10 pts pilot performance, 10 pts character/motivation (30 max)
6. **First non-test-pilots selected** — Aldrin, Anders, Cernan, Chaffee, Cunningham, Schweickart (6 of 14)
7. **Civilian applications accepted** — 228 of 720 applications were civilian

### What Is NOT Yet Clear
- Whether the medical battery was literally identical to Group 2 or had specific additions/deletions for Group 3
- Exact psychological test instruments (MMPI? TAT? Wechsler? — confirmed for Mercury era, not specifically documented for Group 3 in web-accessible sources)
- Duration of the Brooks AFB medical evaluation per candidate (Mercury was ~1 week at Lovelace + Wright-Patterson; Group 3 had the full group phased through July 31 - Aug 15)
- Whether the psychiatric evaluation was still the 30-hour intensive format or had already begun shortening by 1963
- Role of the interview panel (Slayton, Shepard, Schirra, Glenn, North) relative to the medical evaluation — were they purely interview-based or did they review medical results?
