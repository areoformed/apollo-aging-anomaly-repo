# Wikipedia: James A. Abrahamson
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** James Alan Abrahamson
**Born:** May 19, 1933, Williston, North Dakota
**Status:** Living (age 93)
**Military Branch:** United States Air Force
**Final Rank:** Lieutenant General

## Education
- B.S. Aeronautical Engineering, MIT (1955)
- M.S. Aeronautical Engineering, University of Oklahoma (1961)
- Squadron Officer School (1958)
- Air Command and Staff College (1966)
- Industrial College of the Armed Forces (1973)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School, Edwards AFB

## Military Career
- Commissioned through AFROTC (1955)
- Pilot training completed (1957)
- Spacecraft project officer, VELA Nuclear Detection Satellite Program (1961)
- 49 combat missions in Vietnam (428th Tactical Fighter Squadron)
- MOL Group 3 astronaut (1967-1969)
- Manager, Maverick missile program (1971)
- Commander, 4950th Test Wing (1973)
- Director, F-16 Multinational Air Combat Fighter Program (1976-1980)
- Associate Administrator for Space Transport System at NASA (1981-1984) — responsible for Space Shuttle Program
- First Director, Strategic Defense Initiative (1984-1989)
- Promoted to Lieutenant General July 21, 1982
- Retired March 1, 1989

## Post-Military Career
- Executive VP, Hughes Aircraft Company (1989-1992)
- Chairman, Oracle Corporation Board (1992-1995)
- Director and Chairman, ORBIMAGE/GeoEye (1998-2013)

## Space Flights
None (MOL cancelled 1969)

## Awards
- Distinguished Service Medal
- Legion of Merit (3)
- Air Medals (2)
- General Bernard A. Schriever Award (1984)
- Dr. Robert H. Goddard Trophy (1986)
- Honorary doctorates from NYU, Utah State, Rensselaer
- Belgian Order of Leopold, Dutch Order of Orange-Nassau, Norwegian Order of St. Olav
