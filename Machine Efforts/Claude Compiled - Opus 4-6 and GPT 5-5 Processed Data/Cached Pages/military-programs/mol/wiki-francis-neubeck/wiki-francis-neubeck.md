# Wikipedia: Francis G. Neubeck
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** Francis Gregory "Greg" Neubeck
**Born:** April 11, 1932, Washington, D.C.
**Status:** No obituary found; last confirmed alive 2007 (Florida). Would be age 94 if living.
**Military Branch:** United States Air Force
**Rank:** Colonel

## Education
- United States Naval Academy, B.S. (1955)
- Auburn University, MBA (1972)
- U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School (Class 60C)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School (ARPS, Class III)

## Career
- Weapons systems development specialist
- Flight instructor
- Selected as USAF astronaut (November 12, 1965) for MOL Group 1
- Combat tour in Southeast Asia
- Vice commander at Tactical Air Warfare Center, Eglin AFB, Florida
- Retired from Air Force: 1986
- Aerospace industry work
- Author
- 1986 Republican nominee for U.S. House, Florida's 1st District (not elected)
- Resided in Florida as of 2007

## Space Flights
None (MOL cancelled 1969)
