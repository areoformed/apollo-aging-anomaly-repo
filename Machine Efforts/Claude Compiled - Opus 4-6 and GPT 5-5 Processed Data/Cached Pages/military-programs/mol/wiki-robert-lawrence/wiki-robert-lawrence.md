# Wikipedia: Robert H. Lawrence Jr.
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** Robert Henry Lawrence Jr.
**Born:** October 2, 1935, Chicago, Illinois
**Died:** December 8, 1967 (age 32), Edwards Air Force Base, California
**Cause of Death:** F-104 Starfighter crash during flight test training. The aircraft flared too late during descent, struck the ground hard with landing gear failure, caught fire, and rolled. Lawrence was killed when his ejection seat fired sideways and his parachute failed to deploy.
**Military Branch:** United States Air Force
**Rank:** Major

## Education
- Bradley University, B.S. Chemistry (1956)
- Ohio State University, Ph.D. Physical Chemistry (1965)
- U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School (Class 66B, completed June 1967)

## Career
- Designated pilot at age 21 after flight training at Malden AFB
- Instructor pilot in T-33 aircraft for German Air Force
- Senior USAF pilot with 2,500+ flight hours (2,000 in jets)
- F-104 Starfighter tests for spacecraft reentry research
- Selected as MOL Group 3 astronaut (June 1967)

## Historic Distinction
First African-American selected as an astronaut

## Space Flights
None (killed in training before any missions)

## Awards
- Air Force Commendation Medal
- Outstanding Unit Citation

## Honors
- Space Mirror Memorial (1997)
- Bronzeville Walk of Fame plaque
- Cygnus spacecraft NG-13 named in his honor (2020)
- Asteroid Robertlawrence 92892 (2020)
