# Wikipedia: Richard E. Lawyer
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** Richard Earl "Dick" Lawyer
**Born:** November 8, 1932, Los Angeles, California
**Died:** November 12, 2005, Palmdale, California (age 73)
**Cause of Death:** Suspected blood clot (died unexpectedly)
**Military Branch:** United States Air Force
**Rank:** Colonel

## Education
- University of California, Berkeley, B.S. Mechanical Engineering (1955)
- Air Force Aerospace Research Pilot School, Edwards AFB (Class 63A) — received A.B. Honts Award

## Career
- Fighter pilot, two combat tours in Vietnam
- Forward air controller (first tour)
- F-4 Phantom II pilot in Operation Linebacker (second tour)
- Distinguished graduate of Air Force Fighter Weapons School
- Test pilot beginning 1958 (F-105B testing)
- Selected 1965 as USAF MOL astronaut (Group 1)
- Retired from USAF in 1982
- Post-retirement commercial test pilot at Mojave Airport & Spaceport, including work with National Test Pilot School

## Space Flights
None (MOL cancelled 1969)

## Burial
Arlington National Cemetery, January 5, 2006. Survived by wife Gayle, five children, nine grandchildren.
