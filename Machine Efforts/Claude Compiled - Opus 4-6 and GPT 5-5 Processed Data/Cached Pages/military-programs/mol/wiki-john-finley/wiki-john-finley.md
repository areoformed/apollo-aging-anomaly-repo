# Wikipedia: John L. Finley
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** John "Jack" Lawrence Finley
**Born:** December 22, 1935, Winchester, Massachusetts
**Died:** September 19, 2006, Memphis, Tennessee (age 70)
**Cause of Death:** Diabetes and cancer
**Military Branch:** United States Navy
**Rank:** Captain (O-6)

## Education
- United States Naval Academy (graduated 1957)
- Naval flight training at NAS Pensacola (completed August 1958)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School, Edwards AFB (Class 64-A, 1964)

## Military Career
- F-8 Crusader pilot, VF-51 aboard USS Ticonderoga
- Landing Signal Officer, Carrier Air Wing Five
- Vietnam deployment (1968)
- F-4 Phantom II pilot with VF-111 and VX-4
- Commander of Carrier Air Wing Five (1975-1976)
- CO, USS Kawishiwi (1977-1980)
- 3,000+ flight hours, 1,000+ carrier landings

## Space Program
- Selected as USAF astronaut (November 12, 1965) for MOL Group 1
- Resigned as astronaut (April 1968) due to project delays
- No space missions

## Post-Military Career
- Federal Express (15 years), VP Aircraft Line Operations
- Intrepid Aviation Partners, EVP (6 years)
- Dee Howard Aircraft Maintenance, COO/CEO (2 years)
