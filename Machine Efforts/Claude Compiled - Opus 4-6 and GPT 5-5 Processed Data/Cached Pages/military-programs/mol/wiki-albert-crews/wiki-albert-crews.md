# Wikipedia: Albert H. Crews Jr.
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** Albert Hanlin Crews Jr.
**Born:** March 23, 1929, El Dorado, Arkansas
**Died:** June 7, 2025 (age 96)
**Military Branch:** United States Air Force
**Rank:** Colonel

## Education
- University of Louisiana at Lafayette, B.S. Chemical Engineering (1950)
- Air Force Institute of Technology, M.S. Aeronautical Engineering (1959)
- USAF Test Pilot School graduate

## Career
- Selected as military astronaut designee in X-20 Dyna-Soar program (April 20, 1962)
- Assigned as Dyna-Soar pilot (September 20, 1962)
- Selected for Manned Orbiting Laboratory first group (November 12, 1965)
- Transferred to NASA Johnson Space Center, Houston, Texas (June 1969) — hired as test pilot, not astronaut
- Piloted Super Guppy cargo transport, WB-57F atmospheric research aircraft, and Space Shuttle simulator
- Retired at age 65

## Space Flights
None (both Dyna-Soar and MOL cancelled before he could fly)
