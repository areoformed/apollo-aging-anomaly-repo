# Wikipedia: Michael J. Adams
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** Michael James Adams
**Born:** May 5, 1930, Sacramento, California
**Died:** November 15, 1967 (age 37), near Johannesburg, California
**Cause of Death:** Aircraft structural failure during X-15 Flight 191. The craft experienced rapidly increasing aerodynamic pressure at 230,000 feet, entering a violent Mach 5 spin that exceeded the design limits of the aircraft.
**Military Branch:** United States Air Force
**Rank:** Major

## Education
- Sacramento City College (Sacramento Junior College)
- University of Oklahoma, B.S. Aeronautical Engineering (1958)
- Massachusetts Institute of Technology, 18 months astronautics study
- U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School (1962)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School, graduated with honors (December 1963)

## Military Career
- Enlisted USAF (1950)
- Pilot wings and commission (1952, Webb Air Force Base, Texas)
- Fighter-bomber pilot, Korean War: 49 combat missions
- 613th Fighter-Bomber Squadron, England Air Force Base, Louisiana (30 months)
- Rotational duty, Chaumont Air Base, France (6 months)

## Space Program
- Selected for MOL astronaut program (November 1965, Group 1)
- Joined X-15 program (July 1966)
- First X-15 flight: October 6, 1966
- Final flight (X-15 Flight 191): November 15, 1967
- Reached altitude of 266,000 feet (81 km), qualifying as astronaut per U.S. definition

## Awards
- Astronaut Wings (posthumous)
- Air Medal
- Air Force Commendation Medal
- Korean Service Medal

## Memorials
- Space Mirror Memorial, Kennedy Space Center (1991)
- Monument erected near crash site, northwest of Randsburg, California (June 8, 2004)
