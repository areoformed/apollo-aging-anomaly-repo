# Wikipedia: Lachlan Macleay
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** Lachlan "Mac" Macleay
**Born:** June 13, 1931, Saint Louis, Missouri
**Status:** Living (age 94)
**Military Branch:** United States Air Force
**Rank:** Colonel

## Education
- United States Naval Academy, B.S. Electrical Engineering (1954)
- University of Southern California, MBA (1970)
- U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School (Class 60A)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School (ARPS, Class IV)

## Career
- Flight instructor in F-86D aircraft at Moody AFB, Georgia
- Selected as USAF astronaut (November 12, 1965) for MOL Group 1
- Commanded 23rd Tactical Air Support Squadron at Nakhon Phanom, Thailand during Vietnam War
- Flew OV-10 Bronco aircraft as forward air controller
- Retired from USAF May 1, 1978
- Joined Hughes Aircraft in Tucson, Arizona (1978)
- Worked on missile systems development
- Currently resides in Colorado Springs, Colorado

## Space Flights
None (MOL cancelled 1969)
