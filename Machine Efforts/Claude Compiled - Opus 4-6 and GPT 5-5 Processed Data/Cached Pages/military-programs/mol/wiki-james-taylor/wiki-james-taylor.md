# Wikipedia: James M. Taylor
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** James Martin Taylor
**Born:** November 27, 1930, Stamps, Arkansas
**Died:** September 4, 1970 (age 39), Palmdale, California
**Cause of Death:** T-38 Talon aircraft crash during training mission; caused by severe wake turbulence from a C-141 Starlifter performing touch-and-goes on an intersecting runway
**Military Branch:** United States Air Force
**Rank:** Lieutenant Colonel

## Education
- University of Michigan, B.S. Electrical Engineering (1959)
- U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School, Class 63A

## Career
- Selected as USAF astronaut (November 12, 1965) for MOL Group 1
- After MOL cancellation, served as instructor at Test Pilot School
- Served as deputy commandant of Test Pilot School
- Killed in T-38 crash with French Air Force exchange trainee Pierre J. du Bucq

## Space Flights
None (MOL cancelled 1969)

## Memorials
- James M. Taylor Award at Test Pilot School for outstanding graduates of Experimental Test Pilot Course (Phase 1)
- Buried at McChord Air Force Base, Pierce County, Washington
- Survived by wife Jacquelyn and three children
