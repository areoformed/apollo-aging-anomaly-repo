# Wikipedia: Robert T. Herres
## Cached 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** Robert Tralles Herres
**Born:** December 1, 1932, Denver, Colorado
**Died:** July 24, 2008 (age 75), San Antonio, Texas
**Military Branch:** United States Air Force
**Final Rank:** General (four-star)

## Education
- East High School, Denver, Colorado
- B.S., United States Naval Academy (1954)
- M.S. Electrical Engineering, Air Force Institute of Technology (1960)
- M.S. Public Administration, George Washington University (1965)
- Air Command and Staff College (1965)
- Industrial College of the Armed Forces (1971)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School, Edwards AFB (1966)

## Military Career
- Fighter-interceptor pilot and air electronics maintenance roles in Europe
- MOL Group 3 astronaut and Chief, Flight Crew Division, MOL Program (August 1967-1969)
- Commander, 449th Bombardment Wing at Kincheloe AFB (1971-1973)
- Commander, 310th Strategic Wing, U-Tapao Thailand (April-September 1973)
- Commander, Air Force Communications Command, Scott AFB (June 1979)
- Commander, 8th Air Force, Barksdale AFB (July 1981)
- Director for C3 Systems, Joint Chiefs of Staff (October 1982)
- Commander in Chief, Air Force Space Command / NORAD (July 1984)
- Commander in Chief, United States Space Command (September 1985)
- First Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff (1987-1990)
- Promoted to General August 1, 1984

## Post-Military Career
- Chairman and CEO, USAA Group (1993-2002)

## Space Flights
None (MOL cancelled 1969)

## Awards
- Defense Distinguished Service Medal (3)
- Air Force Distinguished Service Medal (2)
- Navy Distinguished Service Medal
- Legion of Merit (2)
- Bronze Star Medal
- Eagle Scout, Distinguished Eagle Scout, Silver Buffalo Award

## Burial
Fort Sam Houston National Cemetery
