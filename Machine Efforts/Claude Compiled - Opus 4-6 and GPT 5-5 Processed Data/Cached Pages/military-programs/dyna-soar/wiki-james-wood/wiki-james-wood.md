# James W. Wood — Wikipedia Cache

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_W._Wood
**Cached:** 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** James Wayne Wood
**Born:** August 9, 1924, Paragould, Arkansas
**Died:** January 1, 1990, Melbourne, Florida (age 65; natural causes)

**Education:**
- BS Aeronautical Engineering, Air Force Institute of Technology, 1954

**Military Service:**
- US Army Air Corps during WWII (10 combat missions)
- Korean War (over 100 combat missions)
- USAF Test Pilot School graduate
- Experimental test pilot at Air Force Flight Test Center, Edwards AFB
- Selected for X-20 Dyna-Soar program, April 1960
- Senior test pilot on Dyna-Soar; slated as pilot for first sub-orbital mission
- Commander of Test Operations at Edwards AFB (until 1978)
- Retired as Colonel, USAF, 1978
- Unsuccessful applicant for NASA Astronaut Group 1 (Mercury Seven)

**Post-Military Career:**
- Test pilot and Director of Operations, Tracor Flight Systems Inc., Newport Beach, California

**Professional Organizations:**
- President of Society of Experimental Test Pilots, 1975
- Fellow of the Society of Experimental Test Pilots

**Family:** Married with three children; considered Pueblo, Colorado his hometown
