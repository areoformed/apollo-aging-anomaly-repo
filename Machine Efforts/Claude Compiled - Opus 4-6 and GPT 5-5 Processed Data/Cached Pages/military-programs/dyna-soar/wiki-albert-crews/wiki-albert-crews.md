# Albert H. Crews Jr. — Wikipedia Cache

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_H._Crews
**Cached:** 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** Albert Hanlin Crews Jr.
**Born:** March 23, 1929, El Dorado, Arkansas
**Died:** June 7, 2025, age 96

**Education:**
- BS Chemical Engineering, University of Louisiana at Lafayette (then University of Southwestern Louisiana), 1950
- MS Aeronautical Engineering, Air Force Institute of Technology, 1959

**Military Service:**
- United States Air Force, retired as Colonel
- USAF Test Pilot School graduate
- Selected for X-20 Dyna-Soar: April 20, 1962 (designee); assigned as pilot September 20, 1962
- Selected for Manned Orbiting Laboratory (MOL): November 12, 1965

**Career:**
- After MOL cancellation (June 1969), transferred to NASA Johnson Space Center
- Piloted Super Guppy cargo transport, WB-57F atmospheric research aircraft, Space Shuttle simulator
- Retired from USAF as Colonel, 1973
- Continued flying for NASA as civilian until retirement at age 65 (~1994)
- Never flew to space

**Family:** Married with three children
**Last surviving MOL astronaut at time of death**
