# William H. Dana — Wikipedia Cache

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_H._Dana
**Cached:** 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** William Harvey Dana
**Born:** November 3, 1930, Pasadena, California
**Died:** May 6, 2014, Phoenix, Arizona (age 83; Parkinson's disease)

**Education:**
- BS, United States Military Academy (West Point), 1952
- MS Aeronautical Engineering, University of Southern California, 1958

**Military Service:**
- Four years as pilot in United States Air Force (post-West Point)

**NASA Career (1958-1998):**
- Joined NASA (then NACA): October 1, 1958
- X-20 Dyna-Soar program: 1960-1962
- X-15 program: 16 flights, top speed 3,897 mph, peak altitude 307,000 feet (~58 miles)
  - Two flights above 50 miles (astronaut-qualifying): X-15 Flight 174 and Flight 197
  - Pilot on final (199th) X-15 flight
  - Awarded NASA civilian astronaut wings retroactively in 2005
- Lifting body vehicles: M2-F1, HL-10, M2-F3, X-24B (31 total missions)
  - Highest lifting body flight: 90,303 feet in HL-10
- Research aircraft: F-100, F-16, F-15, F/A-18
- Chief Engineer, NASA Dryden Flight Research Center, 1993-1998
- Retired 1998 after nearly 40 years of service

**Time in space:** ~20 minutes total across 2 qualifying flights

**Awards:**
- NASA Exceptional Service Medal
- NASA Distinguished Service Medal (1997)
- Haley Space Flight Award, AIAA (1976)
- Milton O. Thompson Lifetime Achievement Award (2000)
- Aerospace Walk of Honor (1993)

**Family:** Married Judi in 1962; four children
