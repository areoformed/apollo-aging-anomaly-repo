# Russell L. Rogers — Wikipedia Cache

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_L._Rogers
**Cached:** 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** Russell Lee Rogers
**Born:** April 12, 1928, Lawrence, Kansas
**Died:** September 13, 1967, Kadena AFB, Okinawa, Japan (age 39; aircraft accident)

**Education:**
- BS Electrical Engineering, University of Colorado, 1958

**Military Service:**
- United States Air Force, rank of Lieutenant Colonel at death
- 142 fighter missions during Korean War
- USAF Test Pilot School graduate
- Experimental test pilot at Edwards AFB
- Key team member testing Northrop T-38 Talon jet trainer
- Selected for X-20 Dyna-Soar program, April 1960
- Served as pilot consultant for Boeing-led program until cancellation December 10, 1963
- Commander of 12th Tactical Fighter Squadron at time of death

**Death:** Engine failure in F-105 fighter plane; ejected but parachute failed to deploy properly. Kadena AFB, Okinawa, September 13, 1967.

**Family:** Married with five children
**Member:** Society of Experimental Test Pilots
