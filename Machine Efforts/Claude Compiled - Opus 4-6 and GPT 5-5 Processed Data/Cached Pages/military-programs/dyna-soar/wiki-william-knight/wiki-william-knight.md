# William J. "Pete" Knight — Wikipedia Cache

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_J._Knight
**Cached:** 2026-05-19

**Full Name:** William John "Pete" Knight
**Born:** November 18, 1929, Noblesville, Indiana
**Died:** May 7, 2004, Los Angeles, California (age 74; leukemia)

**Education:**
- Butler University (attended)
- Purdue University (attended)
- BS Aeronautical Engineering, Air Force Institute of Technology, 1958
- Air Force Experimental Flight Test Pilot School (Class 58C)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School (Class 63A)

**Military Service:**
- United States Air Force, joined 1951, retired as Colonel 1982
- 32 years service, 6,000+ flight hours in 100+ aircraft types
- Test pilot at Edwards AFB from 1958
- Selected for X-20 Dyna-Soar program, 1960
- 16 X-15 flights; set world speed record Mach 6.70 (4,520 mph) on October 3, 1967
- Reached astronaut-qualifying altitude (280,500 ft / 53.1 miles) on October 17, 1967 — earned USAF Astronaut Wings
- Combat pilot in Vietnam: 253 F-100 missions, 1968
- Test director for F-15 Eagle development at Wright-Patterson AFB

**Political Career:**
- Palmdale city council, 1984
- Palmdale mayor, 1988
- California State Assembly, 36th District, 1992-1996
- California State Senate, 17th District, 1996-2004 (died in office)

**Family:** Married Helena Stone; three sons (Steve, Peter, David). Remarried to Gail.
