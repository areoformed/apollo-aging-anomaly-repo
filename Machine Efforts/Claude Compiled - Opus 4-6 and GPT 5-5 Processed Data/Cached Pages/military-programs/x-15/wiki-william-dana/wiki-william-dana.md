# William Harvey Dana — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_H._Dana

- Full Name: William Harvey Dana
- Born: November 3, 1930, Pasadena, California
- Died: May 6, 2014 (age 83), Phoenix, Arizona
- Cause of death: Parkinson's disease
- Military: United States Air Force (4 years active duty as pilot, 1952-1956)
- Education: BS, United States Military Academy (1952); MS Aeronautical Engineering, University of Southern California (1958)
- Pre-X-15: USAF pilot (1952-56); joined NASA Oct 1, 1958 as engineer on X-15 program; progressed to chase pilot and project pilot
- X-15: 16 flights; max altitude 307,000 ft (58.7 mi); max speed Mach 5.53 (3,897 mph); flew final (199th) X-15 flight; USAF astronaut qualification under 50-mile definition
- Post-X-15: Tested lifting-body vehicles and advanced fighter programs; Chief Engineer at NASA Dryden Flight Research Center (1993-1998); nearly 40 years with NASA; continued as contractor/historian after 1998 retirement
- Retroactively awarded NASA civilian astronaut wings in 2005
- Married Judi (1962); four children
- 8,000+ flight hours in 60+ aircraft types
