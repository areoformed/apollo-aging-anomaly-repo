# William John "Pete" Knight — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_J._Knight

- Full Name: William John "Pete" Knight
- Born: November 18, 1929, Noblesville, Indiana
- Died: May 7, 2004 (age 74), Los Angeles, California
- Cause of death: Leukemia
- Military: United States Air Force, Colonel (32 years service, 1951-1982)
- Education: BS Aeronautical Engineering, Air Force Institute of Technology (1958); Air Force Experimental Flight Test Pilot School (Class 58C); Aerospace Research Pilot School (Class 63A)
- Pre-X-15: Test pilot at Edwards AFB on F-100, F-101, F-104, T-38, F-5; selected for X-20 Dyna-Soar (1960, cancelled 1963)
- X-15: 16 flights (Sep 1965 – Sep 1968); set world speed record Mach 6.70 (4,520 mph) on Flight 188 (Oct 3, 1967); reached 280,500 ft on Flight 190; earned USAF astronaut wings
- Post-X-15: Vietnam (253 F-100 combat missions); F-15 test director Wright-Patterson; F-16 test pilot (1979+); retired 1982 with 6,000+ flight hours
- Post-military: Palmdale city council (1984); first elected mayor of Palmdale (1988); California State Assemblyman 36th District (1992-96); State Senator 17th District (1996-2004, until death)
