## X-15 Program Details

- Joined X-15 program: June 1963
- Left X-15 program: 1965
- Total X-15 flights: 16
- Max altitude: 280,500 ft (85.5 km) — exceeded 50 mi on three flights
- Max speed: Mach 5.71
- Earned USAF astronaut wings via X-15 flights
- Youngest pilot to qualify as astronaut at the time
- Final X-15 mission (Flight 153, Oct 14, 1965): first pilot to accomplish sub-orbital spaceflight without MH-96 adaptive flight control system
