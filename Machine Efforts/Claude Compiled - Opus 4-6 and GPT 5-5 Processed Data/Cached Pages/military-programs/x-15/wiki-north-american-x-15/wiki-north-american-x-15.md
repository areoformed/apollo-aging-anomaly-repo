# North American X-15 — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_X-15

## Key Facts

- 12 test pilots flew the X-15 across 199 total flights (1959-1968)
- 8 pilots flew 13 flights exceeding USAF spaceflight criterion (50 miles / 80 km)
- 3 civilian pilots were retroactively awarded NASA astronaut wings in 2005

## Notable Facts

- Joseph A. Walker: Only pilot to exceed 100 km (FAI space boundary); two qualifying flights (July-August 1963)
- William J. Knight: Achieved maximum program speed of Mach 6.70 (4,520 mph) on Flight 188
- Michael J. Adams: Killed in Flight 191 crash (November 15, 1967); posthumously awarded astronaut wings
- Robert A. Rushworth: Most flights of any pilot (34)
- Scott Crossfield: First X-15 pilot; manufacturer's test pilot for North American Aviation
- Forrest S. Petersen: Only Navy pilot in the program
