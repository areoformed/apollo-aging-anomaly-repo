# Michael James Adams — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Adams

- Full Name: Michael James Adams
- Born: May 5, 1930, Sacramento, California
- Died: November 15, 1967 (age 37)
- Cause of death: X-15-3 structural failure during reentry; aircraft broke apart at ~65,000 ft after spin
- Military: United States Air Force, Major
- Education: Sacramento Junior College; BS Aeronautical Engineering, University of Oklahoma (1958); astronautics study at MIT (18 months); USAF Experimental Test Pilot School (1962); Aerospace Research Pilot School (graduated with honors, December 1963)
- Pre-X-15: Korean War fighter-bomber pilot (49 combat missions); 613th Fighter-Bomber Squadron; selected for MOL astronaut program (November 1965)
- X-15: Selected July 1966; 7 flights (Oct 6, 1966 – Nov 15, 1967); peak altitude 266,000 ft (81 km); USAF astronaut qualification
- First American space mission fatality (by USAF 50-mile definition)
- Posthumously awarded USAF astronaut wings
