# Robert Aitken Rushworth — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_A._Rushworth

- Full Name: Robert Aitken Rushworth
- Born: October 9, 1924, Madison, Maine
- Died: March 18, 1993 (age 68), Camarillo, California
- Cause of death: Heart attack
- Military: United States Air Force, Major General
- Education: BE Mechanical Engineering, University of Maine (1951); BS Aeronautical Engineering, Air Force Institute of Technology (1954); National War College (1967)
- Pre-X-15: WWII C-47/C-46 pilot in CBI Theater; Korean War F-80C pilot; Wright-Patterson AFB flight testing; USAF Experimental Flight Test Pilot School (Class 56C, 1957)
- X-15: Selected 1958; 34 flights (Nov 1960 – Jul 1966, most of any pilot); reached 285,000 ft on June 27, 1963 (astronaut qualification); DFC for emergency landing at near-Mach 5
- Post-X-15: Vietnam (189 combat missions, F-4s, Cam Ranh Bay); AGM-65 Maverick program director; Commander 4950th Test Wing; Inspector General AFSC; Commander AFFTC Edwards; Commander AF Test and Evaluation Center; Vice Commander Aeronautical Systems Division; retired June 1, 1981
- Over 6,500 flight hours in 50+ aircraft types
