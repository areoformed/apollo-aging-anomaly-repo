# John Barron McKay — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_B._McKay

- Full Name: John Barron McKay
- Born: December 8, 1922, Portsmouth, Virginia
- Died: April 27, 1975 (age 52), Lancaster, California
- Cause of death: Complications from 1962 X-15 crash injuries (brain damage), compounded by diabetes, alcohol use, and liver disease
- Military: United States Navy, Naval Aviator in WWII Pacific Theater
- Education: BS Aeronautical Engineering, Virginia Polytechnic Institute (1950)
- Pre-X-15: NACA intern (Feb 1951); pilot status (Jul 1952); test pilot on D-558-1, D-558-2, X-1B, X-1E; research pilot on F-100, F-102, F-104, F-107; selected for Man In Space Soonest (1958, unrealized)
- X-15: 29 flights (Oct 1960 – Sep 1966); reached 295,600 ft (90,100 m) on Sep 28, 1965 (USAF astronaut qualification)
- Post-X-15: Continued with NASA until Oct 5, 1971
- Retroactively awarded NASA civilian astronaut wings in 2005 (posthumous)
- Air Medal with two clusters; Presidential Unit Citation
- Married with eight children
