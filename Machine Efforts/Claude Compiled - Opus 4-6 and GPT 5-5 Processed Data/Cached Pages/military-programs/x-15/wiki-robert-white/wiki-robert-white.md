# Robert Michael White — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Michael_White

- Full Name: Robert Michael White
- Born: July 6, 1924, New York City
- Died: March 17, 2010 (age 85), Orlando, Florida
- Cause of death: Age-related causes (died in assisted-living facility)
- Military: United States Air Force, Major General
- Education: BS Electrical Engineering, New York University (1951); MBA, George Washington University (1966)
- Pre-X-15: WWII P-51 pilot, 355th Fighter Group; shot down over Germany on 52nd mission (Feb 1945); POW; Korean War fighter pilot; systems engineer at Rome Air Development Center
- X-15: USAF primary pilot from 1958; 16 flights (April 1960 – December 1962); first pilot to fly Mach 4, 5, and 6; reached 314,750 ft (59.6 mi) on July 17, 1962; first "winged astronaut"
- Post-X-15: Vietnam (70 combat missions, Air Force Cross for Paul Doumer Bridge attack); F-15 program manager; Commander AFFTC Edwards; AFROTC Commandant; Chief of Staff 4th Allied Tactical Air Force; retired Feb 1, 1981
- National Aviation Hall of Fame inducted July 15, 2006 (age 82)
- Aerospace Walk of Honor (1992); American Philosophical Society (1991)
