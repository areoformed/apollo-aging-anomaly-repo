# Joseph Albert Walker — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_A._Walker

- Full Name: Joseph Albert Walker
- Born: February 20, 1921, Washington, Pennsylvania
- Died: June 8, 1966 (age 45), near Barstow, California
- Cause of death: Mid-air collision; F-104N Starfighter collided with XB-70 Valkyrie during formation flight
- Military: United States Army Air Forces / Air Force, Captain
- Education: BA Physics, Washington and Jefferson College (1942)
- Pre-X-15: WWII P-38 pilot (DFC, Air Medal with 7 oak leaf clusters); NACA experimental physicist at Cleveland; transferred to Edwards 1951; 15 years testing experimental aircraft (X-1 variants, Douglas Skystreak/Skyrocket, X-3, X-4, X-5)
- X-15: First NASA pilot; 25 flights (March 1960 – August 1963); only pilot to exceed 100 km (FAI space boundary); two flights above 100 km (July-August 1963); max altitude 354,200 ft (107.9 km); max speed Mach 5.92
- Post-X-15: 35 Lunar Landing Research Vehicle flights beginning October 1964
- Retroactively awarded NASA astronaut wings in 2005
