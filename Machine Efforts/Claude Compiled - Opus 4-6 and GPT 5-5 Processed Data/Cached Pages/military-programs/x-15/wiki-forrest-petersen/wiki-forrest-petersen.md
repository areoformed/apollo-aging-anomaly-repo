# Forrest Silas Petersen — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forrest_S._Petersen

- Full Name: Forrest Silas Petersen
- Born: May 16, 1922, Holdrege, Nebraska
- Died: December 8, 1990 (age 68), Georgetown, South Carolina
- Cause of death: Brain tumor
- Military: United States Navy, Vice Admiral
- Education: BS Naval Science, United States Naval Academy (1944); BS Aeronautical Engineering, Naval Postgraduate School (1950); MS Engineering, Princeton University (1951)
- Pre-X-15: WWII service on USS Caperton (Philippines/Formosa/Okinawa); flight training 1947; Fighter Squadrons 192 and 51; US Naval Test Pilot School (1956), later instructor
- X-15: Assigned Aug 1958 – Jan 1962; 5 free flights; max speed 3,600 mph (Mach 5.3); max altitude ~102,000 ft; only active duty Navy pilot to fly X-15
- Post-X-15: CO Fighter Squadron 154; USS Enterprise XO (1964-66); CO USS Bexar (1967); CO USS Enterprise (1969-71); Combined Task Force 60 commander (1974); Naval Air Systems Command; retired 1980
- Joint Collier Trophy recipient (1962, presented by President Kennedy); NASA Distinguished Service Medal
