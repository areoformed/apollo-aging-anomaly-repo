# Milton Orville Thompson — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Orville_Thompson

- Full Name: Milton Orville Thompson
- Born: May 4, 1926, Crookston, Minnesota
- Died: August 6, 1993 (age 67), Lancaster, California
- Cause of death: Natural causes (details not disclosed)
- Military: United States Naval Reserve, Lieutenant Commander
- Education: BS Engineering, University of Washington (1953)
- Pre-X-15: Navy pilot training at 19; served in China and Japan during WWII; 6 years active naval service; flight test engineer at Boeing (2 years); joined NASA flight research facility; engineer (Mar 1956); research pilot (Jan 1958)
- X-15: 14 flights (Oct 29, 1963 – Aug 1965); max speed 3,712 mph (Mach 5.48); max altitude 214,100 ft; did not qualify as astronaut (below 50 mi)
- Post-X-15: Chief of Research Projects (1969); Chief Engineer at NASA Dryden (1975-1993); NASA Distinguished Service Medal; author of two aviation books
