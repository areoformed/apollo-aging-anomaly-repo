# Albert Scott Crossfield — Wikipedia (cached May 19, 2026)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Scott_Crossfield

- Full Name: Albert Scott Crossfield
- Born: October 2, 1921, Berkeley, California
- Died: April 19, 2006 (age 84), Ludville, Georgia
- Cause of death: Cessna 210A crash in thunderstorm (NTSB: pilot failure to obtain weather info)
- Military: United States Navy, Lieutenant (c. 1943-1946)
- Education: BS Aeronautical Engineering, University of Washington (1949); MS Aeronautical Engineering, University of Washington (1950); Honorary DSc, Florida Institute of Technology (1982)
- Pre-X-15: NACA research pilot at Edwards AFB (1950-1955); first pilot to achieve Mach 2 (November 20, 1953)
- X-15: Chief engineering test pilot for North American Aviation; 14 free flights; first unpowered glide (June 8, 1959); first powered flight (September 17, 1959); last flight December 6, 1960
- Post-X-15: Systems director at NAA (Apollo, Saturn); VP R&D Eastern Air Lines (1967); SVP Hawker Siddeley (1974-75); technical adviser to House Committee on Science and Technology (1977-1993)
- Married Alice Virginia Knoph (1943); one son Paul Stanley Crossfield (b. 1952)
- Buried: Arlington National Cemetery
- Space Grandpa notes: Still flying 200 hrs/year at age 80; trained pilots for Wright Experience 2001-2003; giving speeches (Maxwell AFB) at time of fatal crash at 84
