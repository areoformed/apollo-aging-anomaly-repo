## X-15 Program Details

- First X-15 flight: November 30, 1960
- Last X-15 flight: July 26, 1962
- Total X-15 flights: 7
- Max altitude: 207,000+ ft (63 km) — April 20, 1962
- Max speed: Mach 5.74 (3,989 mph)
- Did NOT qualify as astronaut via X-15 (below 50 mi / 80 km)
- Notable: First flight with Q-ball system; first flight of X-15-3 airframe; first flight of MH-96 adaptive flight control system
- 2,400 total flight hours at departure from Flight Research Center
