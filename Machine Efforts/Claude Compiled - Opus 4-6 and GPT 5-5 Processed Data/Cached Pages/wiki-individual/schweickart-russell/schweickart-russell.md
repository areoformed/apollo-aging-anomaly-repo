# Rusty Schweickart - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Russell Louis Schweickart

**Date of Birth:** October 25, 1935

**Date of Death:** Not deceased (age 90 as of 2026)

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Captain (O-3)
- **Service Period:** 1956-1963 (active duty and Massachusetts Air National Guard)
- Over 4,000 flight hours (3,500 in high-performance jets)

---

## Education
- **BS in Aeronautical Engineering:** MIT (1956)
- **MS in Aeronautics and Astronautics:** MIT (1963)
- Thesis focus: stratospheric radiance

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Research scientist at MIT's Experimental Astronomy Laboratory: atmospheric physics, star tracking, stellar image stabilization

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 1963)

**Mission:**
- **Apollo 9** (March 1969) — Lunar Module Pilot; first in-space test of portable life support system; 1 EVA of 1 hour 17 minutes

**Time in Space:** 10 days, 1 hour

**Other roles:** Backup pilot for Apollo 1; backup commander for Skylab 2; Director of User Affairs, NASA Office of Applications (1974-1977)
**Left NASA:** 1977

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Assistant for science/technology to California Governor Jerry Brown
- California Energy Commission: appointee (5.5 years); chairman (3 years)
- Co-founded Association of Space Explorers (1984-85) with Soviet cosmonauts
- President/CEO, ALOHA Networks, Inc. (1996-1998)
- Co-founded B612 Foundation (2002) for asteroid defense; served as chair then chair emeritus
- Testified before Congress on asteroid threats (May 2005)
- Co-chairman, NASA Advisory Council Task Force on Planetary Defense (2010)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- **Still living at age 90 as of 2026**
- B612 Foundation Chair Emeritus (ongoing)
- Appeared at 2016 Starmus Festival (age 81)
- Photographed publicly in 2022 (age 87)
