# Edward Givens

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Givens
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Edward Galen Givens Jr.

**Date of Birth:** January 5, 1930

**Date of Death:** June 6, 1967 (aged 37)

**Cause of Death:** Automobile accident. Givens was driving his Volkswagen home from a Quiet Birdmen fraternal organization meeting in Pearland, Texas when he missed a sharp, unmarked turn and crashed into a ditch near the Manned Spacecraft Center. He died en route to the hospital.

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Major (at time of death)
- Commissioned as Second Lieutenant (1952)
- Assigned to 35th Fighter-Interceptor Group in Japan (1954–1956)
- Flight instructor and test pilot at multiple bases
- Logged 3,500+ total flight hours; 2,800 hours in jet aircraft

---

## Education
- Texas A&M University (one semester)
- University of Oklahoma (three semesters)
- United States Naval Academy (BS in Naval Sciences, 1952)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Extensive military test pilot and flight instructor experience at Edwards Air Force Base, Naval Air Station Point Mugu, and Aerospace Research Pilot School (graduated 1963).

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as Project Mercury finalist (1959)
- Selected for NASA Astronaut Group 5, "The Original 19" (April 1966)
- Support crew member for Apollo 1 and Apollo 7
- Never assigned to a prime or backup spaceflight crew before his death
