# Vance Brand

- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Vance DeVoe Brand

**Date of Birth:** May 9, 1931

**Date of Death:** Not applicable (living, age 95)

**Birthplace:** Longmont, Colorado, U.S.

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Marine Corps (USMC) and Marine Corps Reserve
- **Rank:** Major, USMC Reserve
- **Service Period:** 1953–1957 (active duty); continued service through 1964 in reserve and Air National Guard
- **Details:** Commissioned officer and naval aviator; served 15-month tour in Japan as jet fighter pilot

---

## Education
- B.S. in Business, University of Colorado at Boulder (1953)
- B.S. in Aeronautical Engineering, University of Colorado at Boulder (1960)
- M.S. in Business Administration, UCLA (1964)
- Naval Test Pilot School graduation (Class 33, 1963)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Employed by Lockheed Corporation (1960–1966):
- Flight test engineer on P-3 Orion aircraft
- Experimental test pilot for F-104 programs (Canadian and German variants)
- Test pilot at West German F-104G Flight Test Center, Istres, France

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as one of "19 pilot astronauts" in April 1966 (NASA Group 5)
- Support crew for Apollo 8 and Apollo 13; CAPCOM during Apollo 13 crisis
- Backup Command Module Pilot for Apollo 15
- Backup commander for Skylab missions 3 and 4
- "Last member of his astronaut class to remain active with NASA"

---

## Missions Flown
1. **Apollo-Soyuz Test Project** (July 15–25, 1975) — Command Module Pilot; 9 days; historic U.S.-Soviet rendezvous; exposed to nitrogen tetroxide gas, briefly lost consciousness
2. **STS-5** (November 11–16, 1982) — Commander; 5 days; first fully operational Shuttle mission
3. **STS-41-B** (February 3–11, 1984) — Commander; 8 days; first untethered EVA with MMU
4. **STS-35** (December 2–10, 1990) — Commander; 9 days; astronomical observatory mission

## Time in Space
31 days, 2 hours, 2 minutes (746 hours); 9,669 total flight hours (8,089 jets)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Departed Astronaut Office (1992)
- Chief of Plans, National Aerospace Plane Joint Program Office at Wright-Patterson AFB
- Assistant Chief of Flight Operations, Dryden Flight Research Center (1994)
- Various leadership: Acting Chief Engineer, Deputy Director for Aerospace Projects, Acting Associate Center Director
- Retired from NASA: January 2008

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- 2019 Philanthropy (age 88): Donated 40 acres of land to Rocky Mountain Conservancy
- Resided actively in Tehachapi, California with wife Beverly Ann Whitnel
- Maintained civic engagement through land conservation initiatives

---
