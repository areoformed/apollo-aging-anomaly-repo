## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Final Rank:** Rear Admiral (upper half)
- **Service Period:** Commissioned 1958; retired 1986
- **Flight Experience:** 7,200 total hours; 5,000 in jet aircraft

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Aeronautical Engineering, Auburn University (1958)
- U.S. Air Force Aerospace Research Pilot School, Edwards Air Force Base
- Naval aviator wings earned 1960

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Served as naval aviator flying Douglas A-1H Skyraider aircraft (1960–1963) aboard USS Saratoga, then transitioned to Douglas A-3B Skywarrior jets with Heavy Attack Squadron Eleven (1963–1965) aboard USS Franklin D. Roosevelt.

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected: NASA Astronaut Group 5 (April 1966)
- Apollo 8: Support crew/CAPCOM role
- Apollo 13: Originally scheduled as Command Module Pilot; removed three days before launch due to German measles exposure; contributed ground support during crisis
- Apollo 16 (April 16–27, 1972): Command Module Pilot; 64 lunar orbits
- STS-4 (June 27 – July 4, 1982): Commander, Space Shuttle Columbia
- STS-51-C (January 24–27, 1985): Commander, Space Shuttle Discovery

---

## Missions Flown
1. **Apollo 16** (1972) — Command Module Pilot
2. **STS-4** (1982) — Commander
3. **STS-51-C** (1985) — Commander

## Time in Space
21 days, 4 hours, 34 minutes; 1 EVA (1 hour, 23 minutes) during Apollo 16 return

---

## Post-NASA Career (1985 onward)
- Director, Grumman Space Station Support Division
- Head of Atlas booster program, General Dynamics (San Diego)
- Vice President, X-33 development program, Lockheed Martin
- Systems Planning and Analysis, Virginia
