# Ed White (Astronaut) - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Edward Higgins White II

**Date of Birth:** November 14, 1930, San Antonio, Texas

**Date of Death:** January 27, 1967 (age 36), Cape Kennedy, Florida

**Cause of Death:** Asphyxiation, smoke inhalation, and thermal burns sustained during Apollo 1 cabin fire during pre-launch testing

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Lieutenant Colonel
- Assigned to 22nd Fighter Squadron at Bitburg Air Base, West Germany (F-86 Sabre and F-100 Super Sabre, 3.5 years)
- Test pilot at Edwards AFB (graduated July 1959)
- Logged over 3,000 flight hours (approximately 2,200 in jets)

---

## Education
- **BS:** United States Military Academy at West Point (1952, ranked 128th of 523)
- **MS:** Aeronautical Engineering, University of Michigan (1959)
- U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School, Edwards AFB (Class 59-C)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Fighter pilot in West Germany
- Test pilot at Wright-Patterson AFB: weapons development flight tests, aircraft design evaluation
- Piloted weightlessness-training aircraft

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 2 (September 17, 1962)

**Missions:**
1. **Gemini 4** (June 3-7, 1965) — Pilot; first American EVA (spacewalk), 36 minutes on June 3, 1965; used Hand-Held Maneuvering Unit
2. **Apollo 1** (planned February 21, 1967) — Senior Pilot; killed in cabin fire during pre-launch test January 27, 1967

---

## Post-NASA Career
Not applicable — died during Apollo 1 testing.
