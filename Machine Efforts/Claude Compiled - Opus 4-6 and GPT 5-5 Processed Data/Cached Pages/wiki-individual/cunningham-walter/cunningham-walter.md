# Walter Cunningham: Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Ronnie Walter Cunningham

**Birth:** March 16, 1932, Creston, Iowa

**Death:** January 3, 2023, Houston, Texas (age 90)

**Cause of Death:** Complications from a fall

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Marine Corps Reserve
- **Rank:** Colonel
- Active Duty: 1953-1956 (fighter pilot, Korean War)
- Reserve Service: 1956-1975
- 54 night-fighter missions in Korea

---

## Education
- Santa Monica College: Associate of Science
- **BA in Physics:** UCLA (1960, with honors)
- **MA in Physics:** UCLA (1961, with distinction)
- Doctoral work at UCLA (ABD) while at RAND Corporation

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Flight training began 1952
- Three years at RAND Corporation as physicist/researcher

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 1963)

**Mission:**
- **Apollo 7** (October 11, 1968) — Lunar Module Pilot; 11-day systems test flight

**Total Time in Space:** 263 hours
**Flight Hours:** 4,500+ total (3,400+ in jets)
**Post-Mission:** Headed Skylab branch at JSC (1968-1971)
**Retirement from NASA:** August 1, 1971

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Harvard Business School Advanced Management Program (1974)
- Published autobiography *The All-American Boys* (1977)
- Radio talk-show host
- Public speaker and technology consultant
- Chairman, Texas Aerospace Commission
- Contributor to *In the Shadow of the Moon* (2007)
- Joined Back to Space organization as Astronaut Consultant (2018)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- Active speaking engagements and public appearances through 2018 (age 86)
- Continued professional consulting work
- Radio broadcasting activities
- Participation in space advocacy organizations
- NASA Distinguished Service Medal (2008, age 76)
- Died at 90 from complications of a fall
