# James Irwin

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Irwin
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** James Benson Irwin

**Date of Birth:** March 17, 1930, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

**Date of Death:** August 8, 1991, Glenwood Springs, Colorado (aged 61)

**Cause of Death:** Heart attack following a bicycle ride

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Colonel
- **Flight hours:** 7,015+ total (5,300+ in jet aircraft)

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Naval Science, United States Naval Academy (1951)
- Master of Science in Aeronautical Engineering, University of Michigan (1957)
- Master of Science in Instrumentation Engineering, University of Michigan (1957)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Test pilot and aeronautical engineer; developmental test pilot for the Lockheed YF-12; chief of Advanced Requirements Branch, Air Defense Command

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected NASA Astronaut Group 5 (April 1966)
- Support crew: Apollo 10
- Backup Lunar Module Pilot: Apollo 12
- Primary mission: Apollo 15 (July 26 – August 7, 1971)

---

## Missions Flown
- **Apollo 15** (July 26 – August 7, 1971) — Lunar Module Pilot

## Time in Space
12 days, 7 hours, 12 minutes; 18 hours 35 minutes on lunar surface; first automobile passenger on the Moon (Lunar Roving Vehicle)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Retired as Colonel (July 31, 1972)
- Founded High Flight Foundation
- Led Mount Ararat expeditions (1973+) seeking Noah's Ark
- Authored books on Christianity and creationism
- Described as "Goodwill Ambassador for the Prince of Peace"

---

## Medical Issues
- Bigeminy detected during Apollo 15 mission
- Pre-flight testing indicated cardiac arrhythmia tendency during strenuous exercise
- Three major heart attacks (first at age 43)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
Not applicable — deceased at age 61.
