# Robert Laurel Crippen - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Robert Laurel Crippen

**Date of Birth:** September 11, 1937 (age 88)

**Date of Death:** Not deceased

**Cause of Death:** N/A

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- Commissioned through Aviation Officer Candidate School
- Served as Naval Aviator from summer 1962 through end of 1964
- Two deployments aboard USS Independence flying A-4 Skyhawks

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** (1960): Aerospace Engineering, University of Texas at Austin
- U.S. Air Force Aerospace Research Pilot School (post-1964)
- High School: New Caney High School, New Caney, Texas (graduated 1955)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Naval Aviator and attack pilot (1962-1964)
- Instructor at Edwards Air Force Base (1965-1966)
- U.S. Air Force Manned Orbiting Laboratory (MOL) program participant (October 1966 onward)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected for NASA Astronaut Group 7 (September 1969) — transferred from cancelled MOL program
- Pilot, STS-1 (April 1981) — first Space Shuttle orbital flight
- Commander of three subsequent Shuttle missions
- Deputy Director, Flight Crew Operations (1984-1986)
- Deputy Director, Shuttle Operations at Kennedy Space Center (July 1987-December 1989)
- Director, Space Shuttle Program at NASA Headquarters (January 1990-January 1992)
- Kennedy Space Center Director (January 1992-January 1995)

---

## Missions Flown
1. **STS-1** (April 12-14, 1981): Pilot; first Space Shuttle orbital flight (Columbia)
2. **STS-7** (June 1983): Commander
3. **STS-41-C** (April 1984): Commander
4. **STS-41-G** (October 1984): Commander

---

## Time in Space
23 days, 13 hours, 46 minutes

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Vice President, Lockheed Martin Information Systems (April 1995-November 1996)
- President, Thiokol Propulsion (December 1996-April 2001)
- Retired to private life in Florida

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- Received Wright Brothers Memorial Trophy (December 15, 2023, age 86) — awarded "for his devotion to public service and advancement of American aerospace"
- Inducted into National Aviation Hall of Fame (2016, age 78)
- Active in professional organizations as fellow member
- Currently living at age 88

---

## Personal Life
From New Caney, Texas. Retired to Florida.
