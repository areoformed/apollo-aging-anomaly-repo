# Clifton Williams - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Clifton Curtis Williams Jr.

**Date of Birth:** September 26, 1932

**Date of Death:** October 5, 1967 (aged 35)

**Cause of Death:** T-38 jet trainer crash near Tallahassee, Florida; mechanical aileron failure caused uncontrollable roll; ejected too late for parachute deployment

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Marine Corps
- **Rank:** Major
- Commissioned August 9, 1954 (Navy ROTC)
- Naval Aviator: August 1956

---

## Education
- Spring Hill College (2 years, studied medicine)
- **BS in Mechanical Engineering:** Auburn University (1954)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Naval aviator with Fleet Marine Force tactical jet squadrons (1956-1961)
- U.S. Naval Test Pilot School graduate (June 1961, Class 28)
- Test pilot, Carrier Suitability Branch, NAS Patuxent River (1961-1963)
- First pilot to land two-seat jet from rear cockpit on aircraft carrier (1962)
- 2,500+ flight hours (2,100+ in jets)

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 18, 1963)
**Roles:** Backup pilot for Gemini 10; selected as LMP for what became Apollo 12

**Missions Flown:** None — killed in T-38 crash before spaceflight

Not applicable — died at age 35.
