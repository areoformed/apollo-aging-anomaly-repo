# Paul Weitz

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_J._Weitz
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Paul Joseph Weitz

**Date of Birth:** July 25, 1932, Erie, Pennsylvania

**Date of Death:** October 22, 2017, Flagstaff, Arizona (aged 85)

**Cause of Death:** Myelodysplastic syndrome

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- Commission: Ensign via Naval ROTC at Penn State
- Earned aviator wings September 1956
- Navy Commendation Medal for Vietnam War combat flights
- Flying hours: Over 7,700 total; 6,400 in jet aircraft

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Aeronautical Engineering, Pennsylvania State University (1954)
- Master of Science in Aeronautical Engineering, Naval Postgraduate School, Monterey, California (1964)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Naval aviator serving in various aircraft squadrons from 1956 until astronaut selection in 1966

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected: NASA Astronaut Group 5, April 1966 (one of 19 selected)
- Skylab 2: Pilot; first crewed Skylab mission; performed critical repairs
- STS-6: Commander; maiden voyage of Space Shuttle Challenger

---

## Missions Flown
1. **Skylab 2** (May 25 – June 22, 1973) — Pilot; 28-day mission; EVA time 2 hours 11 minutes
2. **STS-6** (April 4–9, 1983) — Commander; maiden voyage of Challenger; deployed TDRS-A satellite

## Time in Space
793 hours total

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Retired from NASA May 1994 as Deputy Director, Johnson Space Center
- Brief Navy return in 1976 between NASA assignments
- Lived in Arizona after retirement
