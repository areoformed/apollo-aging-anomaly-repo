# Brian Todd O'Leary - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Brian Todd O'Leary

**Date of Birth:** January 27, 1940, Boston, Massachusetts

**Date of Death:** July 28, 2011 (aged 71), Vilcabamba, Ecuador

**Cause of Death:** Intestinal cancer, shortly after diagnosis

---

## Military Service
No military service documented

---

## Education
- **B.A.** (1961): Physics, Williams College
- **M.A.** (1964): Astronomy, Georgetown University
- **Ph.D.** (1967): Astronomy, University of California, Berkeley
  - Dissertation: "Mars: Visible and Near Infrared Studies and the Composition of the Surface"

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Research associate and assistant professor at Cornell University (1968-1971)
- Deputy team leader, Mariner 10 Venus-Mercury mission at Caltech (1970-1971)
- Teaching positions at San Francisco State, UC Berkeley School of Law, Hampshire College, Princeton, and Cal State Long Beach

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, NASA Astronaut Group 6 (1967)
- Only planetary scientist in NASA Astronaut Corps during Apollo program
- Selected for expertise in Mars atmosphere/surface for planned 1980s human Mars missions
- Resigned April 23, 1968, prior to completing training

---

## Missions Flown
None (resigned before completing training)

---

## Time in Space
Zero

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Worked at Science Applications International Corporation; left in 1987 refusing military applications
- From late 1980s onward, lectured at alternative institutions including Findhorn Foundation, Esalen Institute, and Omega Institute
- Co-founded Montesueños Eco-Retreat in Vilcabamba, Ecuador (2008)
- Political advisor to presidential candidates Morris Udall, Jesse Jackson, Dennis Kucinich, George McGovern, and Walter Mondale
- Published 8 books on astronomy, space, and unconventional science topics

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
Not applicable — O'Leary died at age 71 from intestinal cancer. He did not reach age 80.
