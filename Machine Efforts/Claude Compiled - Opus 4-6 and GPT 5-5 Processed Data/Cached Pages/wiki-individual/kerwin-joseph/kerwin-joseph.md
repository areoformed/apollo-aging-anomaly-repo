# Joseph Kerwin

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_P._Kerwin
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 4 (The Scientists, 1965)

---

**Full Name:** Joseph Peter Kerwin

**Date of Birth:** February 19, 1932, Oak Park, Illinois

**Date of Death:** Still living (age 94)

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy Medical Corps
- **Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- **Commission:** July 1958
- **Flight surgeon designation:** December 1958
- **Flight hours:** 4,500 hours

---

## Education
- College of the Holy Cross: BA in Philosophy with pre-med minor (1953), magna cum laude, ranked 8th in class of 380
- Northwestern University (Feinberg School of Medicine): MD (1957)
- District of Columbia General Hospital: internship
- Naval Air Station Pensacola: School of Aviation Medicine
- Flight surgeon wings earned: 1962, Beeville, Texas

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Navy pilot and flight surgeon at time of astronaut selection

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected: NASA Astronaut Group 4, June 1965
- CAPCOM (capsule communicator) for Apollo 13, 1970
- Science Pilot, Skylab 2 mission
- Director of Space and Life Sciences, Johnson Space Center (1984–1987)
- Issued 1986 report on Challenger disaster crew deaths

---

## Missions Flown
- **Skylab 2:** May 25 – June 22, 1973

## Time in Space
28 days, 0 hours, 50 minutes; 1 EVA of 3 hours 23 minutes

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Lockheed (1987): managed Extravehicular Systems Project; co-invented SAFER device
- Systems Research Laboratories (1996): Program Manager
- KRUG Life Sciences (1997): president
- Wyle Laboratories (1998–2004): Life Sciences division work
- NSBRI Board of Directors: industry representative

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- Published co-authored book "Homesteading Space" (2008)
- Authored novel "Finding Eden, A Prehistorical Novel" (2024, age 92)
- Appeared in documentary film "Searching for Skylab" (2018, age 86)
- Resides in College Station, Texas with family
