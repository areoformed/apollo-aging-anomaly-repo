# Charles Gordon Fullerton - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Charles Gordon Fullerton

**Date of Birth:** October 11, 1936, Rochester, New York

**Date of Death:** August 21, 2013 (aged 76), Lancaster, California

**Cause of Death:** Complications from a stroke suffered in 2009

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Colonel
- **Service Period:** July 1958 - July 1988 (30 years)
- F-86 interceptor pilot
- B-47 bomber pilot at Davis-Monthan AFB
- Test pilot (Wright-Patterson AFB)
- Manned Orbiting Laboratory (MOL) program, 1966-1969

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** (1957): Mechanical Engineering, California Institute of Technology
- **Master of Science** (1958): Mechanical Engineering, California Institute of Technology
- High School: Ulysses S. Grant High School, Portland, Oregon (1953)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Mechanical design engineer at Hughes Aircraft Company, Culver City, California (prior to July 1958)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected for NASA Astronaut Group 7 (September 1969) — transferred from cancelled MOL program
- Support crews for Apollo 14, 15, 16, and 17
- Piloted Space Shuttle prototype Enterprise during Approach and Landing Tests (ALT, 1977)

---

## Missions Flown
1. **STS-3** (March 22-30, 1982): Pilot, orbital flight test mission on Columbia; tested Canadarm and thermal stress; landed at White Sands, New Mexico
2. **STS-51-F** (July 29 - August 6, 1985): Commander, Spacelab 2 mission aboard Challenger; first pallet-only Spacelab mission carrying 13 major experiments

---

## Time in Space
15 days, 22 hours, 50 minutes

---

## Post-NASA Career
- November 1986 onward: Research pilot at NASA Dryden Flight Research Facility, Edwards, California
- Key research programs: Landing Systems Research Aircraft (Convair 990), F-18 Systems Research Aircraft, Propulsion Controlled Aircraft (landed F-15 and MD-11 using engine thrust only)
- Additional programs: C-140 Jetstar, F-111, F-14, X-29, B-52 launch aircraft support
- Tu-144LL supersonic aircraft (joint US-Russia program)
- Total flying experience: Over 16,000 hours; piloted 135 different aircraft types
- Retired December 31, 2007

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
Not applicable — Fullerton died at age 76. He suffered a stroke in 2009 (age 72) and was cared for in a facility in Lancaster, California until his death in 2013. No evidence of active professional work post-stroke.
