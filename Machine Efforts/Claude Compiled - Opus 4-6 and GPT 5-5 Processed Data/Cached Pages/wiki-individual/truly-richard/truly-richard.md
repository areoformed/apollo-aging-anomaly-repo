# Richard Harrison Truly - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Richard Harrison Truly

**Date of Birth:** November 12, 1937, Fayette, Mississippi

**Date of Death:** February 27, 2024 (age 86), Genesee, Colorado

**Cause of Death:** Not specified in article

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Vice Admiral
- **Years of Service:** 1959-1989
- Notable Command: Fighter Squadron 33 (VF-33)
- Aircraft: F-8 Crusaders (300+ carrier landings aboard USS Intrepid and USS Enterprise)

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** (1959): Aeronautical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology
- U.S. Air Force Aerospace Research Pilot School (1963-1965, Edwards AFB, California)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Naval aviator and fighter pilot (1960 onwards)
- Test pilot and instructor at Edwards AFB
- Selected for USAF Manned Orbiting Laboratory program (1965)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected for NASA Astronaut Group 7 (August 1969) — transferred from cancelled MOL program
- Astronaut support crew and CAPCOM for Skylab missions (1973) and Apollo-Soyuz (1975)
- Approach and Landing Tests pilot (1977) — flew Space Shuttle Enterprise
- First man to be launched into space on his birthday (STS-2, November 12, 1981)
- Associate Administrator for Space Flight (February 1986) — oversaw 31-month recovery following Challenger disaster
- 8th NASA Administrator (July 1, 1989 - March 31, 1992)

---

## Missions Flown
1. **Approach and Landing Tests** (1977): Pilot, Space Shuttle Enterprise
2. **STS-2** (November 12-14, 1981): Pilot; second Space Shuttle orbital flight
3. **STS-8** (August 30 - September 5, 1983): Commander; first night launch and landing

## Post-NASA Career
- Georgia Tech Research Institute (1992-1997): Vice President and Director

- Colorado School of Mines Board of Trustees (2010): Appointed by Governor Bill Ritter; served as vice chairman
