# Jim Lovell: Biographical Data

**Full Name:** James Arthur Lovell Jr.

**Birth:** March 25, 1928, Cleveland, Ohio

**Death:** August 7, 2025, Lake Forest, Illinois (aged 97)

**Cause of Death:** Not specified in source material

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- **Service Period:** 1952-1973
- Naval aviator, designated February 1, 1954
- Fighter pilot (McDonnell F2H Banshee), 1954-1956
- Test pilot training, Naval Air Test Center, 1958
- F-4 Phantom II program manager, 1960

---

## Education
- University of Wisconsin-Madison (2 years, engineering, "Flying Midshipman" program, 1946-1948)
- United States Naval Academy (BS, graduated spring 1952)
- Naval Air Test Center (test pilot training, Class 20, graduated top of class)
- Aviation Safety School, University of Southern California

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Flight test engineer and weapons systems specialist at Naval Air Test Center; F-4 Phantom program management; naval aviation training officer.

---

## NASA Career Highlights

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 2 (September 1962)

**Missions:**
1. **Gemini 7** (December 4-18, 1965) — Pilot with Frank Borman; 14-day endurance mission
2. **Gemini 12** (November 11-15, 1966) — Command pilot with Buzz Aldrin
3. **Apollo 8** (December 21-27, 1968) — Command Module Pilot; first crewed lunar orbit
4. **Apollo 13** (April 11-17, 1970) — Commander; oxygen tank failure; safe return via lunar gravity-assist

**Time in Space:** 29 days, 19 hours, 5 minutes total

**Distinctions:**
- First person to fly into space four times
- First person to reach the Moon twice (but never landed)
- Apollo 13 crew achieved farthest human distance from Earth

---

## Post-NASA Career (Retired March 1, 1973)
- CEO, Bay-Houston Towing Company (1975)
- President, Fisk Telephone Systems (1977)
- Executive Vice President, Centel Corporation, Chicago (retired January 1, 1991)
- Board member: Federal Signal Corporation (1984-2003), Astronautics Corporation of America (1990-1999)
- Co-founder/owner, Lovell's of Lake Forest restaurant (1999); sold 2006; closed 2015
- Co-authored *Lost Moon: The Perilous Voyage of Apollo 13* (1994)
- Film cameo: *Apollo 13* (1995) as USS Iwo Jima captain

---

**Public Appearances and Speaking:**
- Spoke at North Carolina Science Festival (April 2017, age 89)
- Honorary doctorate, University of Wisconsin-Madison (December 2016, age 88)
