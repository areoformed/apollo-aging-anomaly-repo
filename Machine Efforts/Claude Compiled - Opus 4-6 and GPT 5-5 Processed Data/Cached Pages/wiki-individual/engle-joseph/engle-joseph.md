# Joseph Engle

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Engle
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Joseph Henry Engle

**Date of Birth:** August 26, 1932, Abilene, Kansas

**Date of Death:** July 10, 2024, Houston, Texas (age 91)

**Cause of Death:** Not specified in article

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force (USAF) and Air National Guard (ANG)
- **Ranks:** Colonel (USAF, retired 1986); Major General (ANG, retired ~1991)

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Aerospace Engineering, University of Kansas (1955)
- Air Force Reserve Officer Training Corps commissioning
- Air Force Test Pilot School, Class 61C (1961)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School, Class III

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- F-100 Super Sabre pilot with 474th and 309th Fighter Squadrons at George Air Force Base
- Test pilot, Fighter Test Group, Edwards Air Force Base
- X-15 research program test pilot (June 1963 onwards)
- Logged 15,400+ flight hours; flew 185+ aircraft types

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected NASA Astronaut Group 5 (April 1966)
- Support crew: Apollo 10
- Backup Lunar Module Pilot: Apollo 14
- Originally scheduled for Apollo 17 moon landing, replaced by geologist Harrison Schmitt
- Commanded Approach and Landing Tests (1977)
- STS-2 commander (1981) — "the last NASA rookie to command a spaceflight until Raja Chari in 2021"
- STS-51-I commander (1985)
- Deputy Associate Administrator, NASA Headquarters (March–December 1982)
- Participated in Challenger disaster investigation (1986)

---

## Missions Flown
- X-15 Flights 138, 143, 153 (three sub-orbital space flights exceeding 50 miles altitude)
- ALT, Crew 2 (Space Shuttle Enterprise approach/landing tests)
- STS-2
- STS-51-I

## Time in Space
9 days, 8 hours, 30 minutes (225+ hours total)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Retired USAF: November 14, 1986
- Retired NASA: November 28, 1986
- Kansas Air National Guard: December 1, 1986 (brigadier general initially); promoted to major general (1989)
- ANG Assistant to Commander in Chief, U.S. Space Command/NORAD at Peterson AFB, Colorado
- Retired from ANG around 1991

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- 2001: Inducted into National Aviation Hall of Fame and U.S. Astronaut Hall of Fame
- 2007: Received Award of Air Force Space and Missile Pioneers
- 2009: Became Fellow, Society of Experimental Test Pilots
- 2014: Inducted into International Air & Space Hall of Fame (age 82)
- Active in interviews and public appearances through his 80s and early 90s
- Survived to age 91
