# Owen Garriott

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Garriott
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 4 (The Scientists, 1965)

---

**Full Name:** Owen Kay Garriott

**Birth:** November 22, 1930, in Enid, Oklahoma

**Death:** April 15, 2019, at his home in Huntsville, Alabama (age 88)

**Cause of Death:** Not specified in the document

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Service Period:** 1953–1956
- **Position:** Electronics officer

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** in Electrical Engineering, University of Oklahoma (1953)
- **Master of Science** in Electrical Engineering, Stanford University (1957)
- **Ph.D.** in Electrical Engineering, Stanford University (1960)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Electronics officer, U.S. Navy (1953–1956)
- Assistant and associate professor of electrical engineering, Stanford University (1961–1965)
- Research focus: ionospheric physics; authored/co-authored 45+ scientific papers

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as one of six "scientist-astronauts" in NASA Astronaut Group 4 (1965)
- **Skylab 3 Mission** (July–September 1973): 60 days aboard Skylab space station; conducted extensive experiments on the Sun, Earth resources, and life sciences
- **STS-9/Spacelab-1 Mission** (November–December 1983): 10 days aboard Space Shuttle Columbia; over 70 experiments across six disciplines
- First amateur radio operator in space; made first amateur radio contact from space on December 1, 1983, using "W5LFL" call sign
- Held NASA fellowship in Space Station Project Office between missions

---

## Missions & Time in Space
- **Total Time in Space:** 69 days, 18 hours, 56 minutes
- **Total EVAs:** 3
- **Total EVA Time:** 13 hours, 43 minutes
- **Retirement from NASA:** June 1986

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Vice president of space programs, Teledyne Brown Engineering (January 1988–May 1993)
- Adjunct professor in Structural Biology Laboratory, University of Alabama in Huntsville
- Research on extremophile microbes from alkaline lakes, deep-sea hydrothermal vents, and Antarctic expeditions
- Co-founder, Enid Arts and Sciences Foundation (1992)
- Established Garriott Family Foundation 501(c)(3) for philanthropic adventure travel

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- Active as adjunct professor conducting microbiology research
- Participated in multiple international diving expeditions and Antarctic trips
- Co-authored "Homesteading Space" (2008, when he was 77–78)
- Attended his son Richard's space launch at Baikonur Cosmodrome in 2008
- Involvement with professional boards and the Astronaut Scholarship Foundation (as vice president and vice chairman)

---

## Notable Achievement
His son Richard Garriott became "the first American and the second person worldwide to follow a parent into space" via Soyuz TMA-13 (October 2008).
