# Donald Lee Holmquest - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Donald Lee Holmquest

**Date of Birth:** April 7, 1939

**Date of Death:** Not deceased (living, age 87)

**Cause of Death:** N/A

---

## Military Service
None documented. Flight training completed at Williams Air Force Base, Arizona, where he "earned his Air Force wings" and logged 750 hours in jet aircraft during NASA career.

---

## Education
- **B.S.** (1962): Electrical Engineering, Southern Methodist University (highest honors)
- **M.D.** (1967): Baylor College of Medicine
- **Ph.D.** (1968): Physiology, Baylor College of Medicine
  - Dissertation: "Thermal Rhythms in the Rat: The Influence of Environmental and Physiological Factors as Studied by Telemetry"
- **J.D. cum laude** (1988): University of Houston Law Center

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Internal medicine residency at Houston Methodist Hospital

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, NASA Astronaut Group 6 ("XS-11"), 1967
- Flight training at Williams Air Force Base, Arizona
- Worked 1.5 years on Skylab habitability systems and medical experiments
- Leave of absence May 1971; formal resignation September 1973
- Reason for departure: Not assigned to prime or backup Skylab crew; returned to Baylor for nuclear medicine training

None

## Time in Space
Zero

## Post-NASA Career
- Chief of Nuclear Medicine, Ben Taub General Hospital, Houston
- Department founder, Eisenhower Medical Center, California (Nuclear Medicine)
- Associate Dean of Medicine, Texas A&M University College of Medicine
- Senior Partner, Wood, Lucksinger & Epstein law firm
- Founder, Holmquest & Associates (healthcare law focus)
- CEO, California Regional Health Information Organization (RHIO)
- Adjunct Professor of Medicine, Baylor College of Medicine (concurrent with legal practice)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
None specifically documented in the Wikipedia article. Currently living at age 87, residing in Menlo Park, California.

---

## Notable
Holmquest holds four advanced degrees (BS, MD, PhD, JD) — one of the most credentialed astronaut candidates ever selected.
