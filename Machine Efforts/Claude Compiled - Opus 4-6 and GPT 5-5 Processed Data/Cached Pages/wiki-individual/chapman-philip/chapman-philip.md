# Philip K. Chapman - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Philip Kenyon Chapman

**Date of Birth:** March 5, 1935, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia

**Date of Death:** April 5, 2021 (aged 86), Scottsdale, Arizona

**Cause of Death:** Not specified in Wikipedia article

---

## Military Service
Royal Australian Air Force Reserve, 1953-1955 (learned to fly De Havilland Tiger Moth during National Service)

---

## Education
- **B.S.** (1956): Physics & Mathematics, University of Sydney
- **M.S.** (1964): Aeronautics & Astronautics, MIT
- **Sc.D.** (1967): Instrumentation, MIT
  - Doctoral advisors: Steven Weinberg and Rainer Weiss (both Nobel Laureates)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Philips Electronics Industries, Sydney (1956-1957)
- Auroral/radio physicist, Mawson Station Antarctica (1958)
- Electro-optics engineer, Canadian Aviation Electronics (1960-1961)
- Staff physicist, MIT's Experimental Astronomy Lab (until 1967)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, NASA Group 6 (August 1967)
- Support crew, Apollo 14 (Mission Scientist)
- Resigned July 1972, citing disagreement with Space Shuttle program decisions
- Notable quote on exit: "It appears that we have to make a choice between losing our competency as pilots or losing our competency as scientists."

---

## Missions Flown
None (resigned before any spaceflights)

---

## Time in Space
Zero

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Laser propulsion work, Avco Everett Research Laboratory (1972-1977)
- Solar power satellite development, Arthur D. Little
- President, L5 Society (mid-1980s)
- Citizens' Advisory Council on National Space Policy (advised multiple presidents)
- Led Antarctic mineral resources expedition (1989)
- Echo Canyon Software president (1989-1994)
- Chief Scientist, Rotary Rocket (1998)
- Presented papers, 55th International Astronautical Congress (2004)
- Chief Scientist, Transformational Space Corporation/t/Space
- Founded Solar High Study Group (2009)
- Presented slides to US Air Force on space-based solar power (July 2010)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
Chapman died at 86. Evidence of continued activity into his 70s includes presenting at conferences (2004, age 69), founding Solar High Study Group (2009, age 74), and presenting to the US Air Force (2010, age 75). No specific evidence of activity past age 80 documented in the Wikipedia article.

---

## Personal Life
Australian-born, became a naturalized US citizen (first Australian-born astronaut selected by NASA).
