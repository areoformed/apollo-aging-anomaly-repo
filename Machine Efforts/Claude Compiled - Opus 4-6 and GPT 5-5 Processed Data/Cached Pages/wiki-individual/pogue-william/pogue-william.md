# William Pogue

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Pogue
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** William Reid Pogue

**Date of Birth:** January 23, 1930

**Date of Death:** March 3, 2014 (aged 84)

**Cause of Death:** Natural causes at home in Cocoa Beach, Florida

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force (USAF)
- **Rank:** Colonel
- **Service Duration:** 1951–1975 (24 years)
- **Combat Experience:** 43 missions during Korean War (1953–1954)
- **Special Assignments:** USAF Thunderbirds aerobatics pilot (1955–1957); RAF exchange program as test pilot (1963–1965)

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Education, Oklahoma Baptist University (1951)
- Master of Science in Mathematics, Oklahoma State University (1960)
- Empire Test Pilots' School, Farnborough, England (graduated 1965)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Mathematics professor at U.S. Air Force Academy (1960–1963); test pilot; flight instructor; qualified in 50+ aircraft types

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected in NASA Astronaut Group 5 (April 1966)
- Support crew: Apollo 7, 11, 13, and 14
- Skylab 4 pilot (November 16, 1973 – February 8, 1974)
- Duration record: 84 days, 1 hour, 15 minutes — longest crewed flight until 1978
- 2 EVAs totaling 13 hours, 34 minutes
- Retired September 1, 1975

---

## Missions Flown
- **Skylab 4** (November 16, 1973 – February 8, 1974) — Pilot

## Time in Space
84 days, 1 hour, 15 minutes

## Post-NASA Career
- Authored _How Do You Go to the Bathroom in Space?_ (1985)
- Co-wrote science-fiction novel _The Trikon Deception_ with Ben Bova (1992)
- Consultant for Boeing and Martin Marietta on space station technology
- "Continuously presented lectures over a 40-year career, working at more than 500 schools and 100 civic clubs"

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- Published autobiography _But for the Grace of God_ (March 2011, age 80)
- Third marriage in 2012 (age 82)
- Continued lecture circuit into his 80s
