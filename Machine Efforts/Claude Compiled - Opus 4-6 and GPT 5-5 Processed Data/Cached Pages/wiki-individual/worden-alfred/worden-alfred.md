# Alfred Worden

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Worden
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Alfred Merrill Worden

**Date of Birth:** February 7, 1932

**Date of Death:** March 18, 2020 (age 88)

**Cause of Death:** Infection; hospitalized at Texas Medical Center in Houston; died at assisted living facility in Sugar Land, Texas

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Colonel
- **Service Period:** 1955–1975

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Military Science, United States Military Academy at West Point (1955)
- Master of Science in Aerospace Engineering, University of Michigan (1963)
- Master of Science in Instrumentation Engineering, University of Michigan (1963)
- Honorary Doctor of Science in Astronautical Engineering, University of Michigan (1971)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Primary flight training at Moore Air Force Base, Texas
- Fighter pilot with 95th Fighter Interceptor Squadron, Andrews Air Force Base (1957–1961)
- Training at Empire Test Pilots' School, Farnborough, England
- Instructor at Aerospace Research Pilot School (ARPS); graduated September 1965

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected to NASA Astronaut Group 5 (April 1966)
- Support crew for Apollo 9
- Backup Command Module Pilot for Apollo 12
- Apollo 15: Command Module Pilot; orbited the Moon 74 times
- First deep-space EVA: 38 minutes during return journey
- Listed in Guinness Book of World Records as "most isolated human being" — 2,235 miles from crewmates while orbiting Moon alone
- Senior Aerospace Scientist and Chief of Systems Study Division at Ames Research Center (1973–1975)

---

## Missions Flown
- **Apollo 15** (July 26 – August 7, 1971) — Command Module Pilot

## Time in Space
12 days, 7 hours, 12 minutes (295 hours 11 minutes)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Founded Alfred M. Worden, Inc.
- Director of Energy Management Programs, Northwood Institute, Midland, Michigan
- Ran for U.S. House of Representatives, Florida's 12th District (1982) — lost Republican primary
- Executive positions with Jet Electronics and Technology, Inc. and B.F. Goodrich
- Retired from business world (1996)
- Chair of Astronaut Scholarship Foundation board (2005–2011)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- Autobiography _Falling to Earth_ made Los Angeles Times Bestseller top 12 (2011, age 79)
- Astronaut Consultant with Back to Space organization (2018, age 86)
- Technical consultant to film _First Man_ (2018, age 86)
- Public appearance at SXSW conference (March 2019, age 87)
- Made numerous public appearances promoting space exploration and STEM education
- Described as "one of the most approachable" former astronauts, "ready to chat"
