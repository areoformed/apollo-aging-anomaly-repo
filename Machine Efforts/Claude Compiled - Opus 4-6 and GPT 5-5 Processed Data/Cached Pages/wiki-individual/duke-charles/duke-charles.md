# Charles Duke

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Duke
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Charles Moss Duke Jr.

**Date of Birth:** October 3, 1935, Charlotte, North Carolina

**Date of Death:** Not deceased (age 90)

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force (USAF)
- **Rank:** Brigadier General (retired 1986)
- Commissioned as Second Lieutenant (1957)
- Fighter pilot with 526th Fighter-Interceptor Squadron at Ramstein Air Base, West Germany (1959–1962)

---

## Education
- B.S. in Naval Sciences, United States Naval Academy (1957)
- M.S. in Aeronautics and Astronautics, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (1964)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Test pilot and instructor at Aerospace Research Pilot School; taught control systems in F-101 Voodoo, F-104 Starfighter, and T-33 Shooting Star

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected for NASA Astronaut Group 5 (April 1966)
- Support crew for Apollo 10 (1969)
- CAPCOM for Apollo 11 lunar landing
- Backup Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 13
- Prime Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 16 (1972)

---

## Missions Flown
- **Apollo 16** (April 1972) — Lunar Module Pilot

## Time in Space
11 days, 1 hour, 51 minutes total; 3 EVAs (21 hours 38 minutes)

**Lunar Achievement:** Tenth person and youngest to walk on the Moon at age 36 years, 201 days (April 21, 1972)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Air Force Reserve service (1976–1986)
- Coors distributorship in San Antonio, Texas (1975–1978)
- Real estate ventures with Ken Campbell
- Corporate positions: Orbit Corporation president (1976–1978); Robbins Company director (1986–1989); Duke Resources chairman (1988–1993)
- Consultant for Lockheed Martin
- Active in Astronaut Scholarship Foundation (chairman 2011–2012)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- Featured in documentary _Lunar Tribute_ (premiered October 2017, age 82)
- Appeared at Explor'Espace festival in Montrouge, France (November 6, 2021, age 86)
- Named Texan of the Year for 2020 (December 2019, age 84)
- Active in Anglican Church ministry and young Earth creationism advocacy
- Continues public speaking and appearances at space-related events
