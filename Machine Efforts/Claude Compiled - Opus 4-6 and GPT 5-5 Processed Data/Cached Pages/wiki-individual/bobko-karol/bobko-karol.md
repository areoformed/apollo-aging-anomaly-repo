# Karol Joseph "Bo" Bobko - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Karol Joseph "Bo" Bobko

**Date of Birth:** December 23, 1937, Queens, New York

**Date of Death:** August 17, 2023, Half Moon Bay, California (age 85)

**Cause of Death:** Complications of an unspecified degenerative disease of the nervous system

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Colonel (USAF, Retired)
- **Flight Hours:** Over 6,600 hours in F-100, F-104, F-105, T-33, T-38, and other aircraft
- **Combat Service:** 1961-1965, flew F-100 and F-105 aircraft with 523rd and 336th Tactical Fighter Squadrons
- **Retirement from USAF:** January 1, 1989

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** (1959): Aerospace Engineering, United States Air Force Academy
- **Master of Science** (1970): University of Southern California
- Secondary: Brooklyn Technical High School (graduated 1955)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Pilot training completion (1960)
- Assigned to Aerospace Research Pilot School at Edwards Air Force Base
- USAF Manned Orbiting Laboratory (MOL) program astronaut (1966)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected for NASA Astronaut Group 7 (September 1969) — transferred from cancelled MOL program
- Participated in Skylab Medical Experiment Altitude Test (SMEAT)
- Support crew, Apollo-Soyuz Test Project (1975)
- Support crew, Space Shuttle Approach and Landing Tests
- Lead astronaut, test and checkout group for Space Shuttle Columbia (STS-1)
- First graduate of the U.S. Air Force Academy to travel in space
- First astronaut to fly three different space shuttles on three different missions
- Only astronaut to have flown on the maiden flights of two Space Shuttles

---

## Missions Flown
1. **STS-6** (April 4-9, 1983): Pilot, Space Shuttle Challenger maiden voyage; deployed TDRS-1; first Shuttle spacewalk; 120 hours
2. **STS-51-D** (April 12-19, 1985): Mission Commander, Space Shuttle Discovery; deployed two communications satellites; first unscheduled spacewalk; 168 hours
3. **STS-51-J** (October 3-7, 1985): Mission Commander, Space Shuttle Atlantis maiden voyage; DoD classified payloads; 98 hours

---

## Time in Space
16 days, 2 hours, 3 minutes (386 hours total)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- 1988: Retired from NASA and Air Force; joined Booz Allen Hamilton (Houston) as principal managing human spaceflight efforts
- 2000: Vice President for Strategic Programs at SPACEHAB, Inc. (Houston)
- 2005: Program Manager for NASA Ames Research Center Simulation Laboratories contract at SAIC

No specific evidence of active professional engagement, public speeches, flying, or interviews after age 80 documented in the Wikipedia article. Resided with his wife in Half Moon Bay, California until death at 85 from a degenerative neurological disease.
