# William Anders - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** William Alison Anders

**Date of Birth:** October 17, 1933 (British Hong Kong)

**Date of Death:** June 7, 2024 (age 90)

**Cause of Death:** Aircraft crash in Beechcraft T-34 Mentor near San Juan Islands, Washington — nosedive into Puget Sound waters

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force (USAF)
- **Final Rank:** Major General, USAF Reserve (retired 1988)
- Commissioned as Second Lieutenant (1955)
- Fighter pilot: F-89 Scorpions and F-101 Voodoos

---

## Education
- **BS in Electrical Engineering:** U.S. Naval Academy (1955)
- **MS in Nuclear Engineering:** Air Force Institute of Technology (1962)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Fighter pilot and interceptor pilot (1956-1963)
- Nuclear reactor program manager at Air Force Weapons Laboratory

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (1963)

**Missions:**
1. **Apollo 8** (December 1968) — Lunar Module Pilot; first crewed lunar orbit; photographed iconic "Earthrise" image (December 24, 1968)

**Time in Space:** 6 days, 3 hours
**Backup Crew:** Apollo 11 (backup CMP)
**Retired from NASA:** September 1, 1969

---

## Post-NASA Career

**Government Service:**
- Executive Secretary, National Aeronautics and Space Council (1969-1973)
- Commissioner, U.S. Atomic Energy Commission (1973-1975)
- First Chairman, Nuclear Regulatory Commission (1975-1976)
- U.S. Ambassador to Norway (1976-1977)

**Corporate Career:**
- General Electric: VP/GM Nuclear Products Division (1977-1980); GM Aircraft Equipment Division (1980-1984)
- Textron: EVP for Aerospace (1984-1986); Senior EVP for Operations (1986+)
- General Dynamics: Vice Chairman (1990); Chairman and CEO (1991-1993); Chairman until 1994

**Heritage Flight Museum:**
- Founded 1996; served as President until 2008

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- Maintained active pilot status; logged over 8,000 flight hours total
- Piloted North American P-51 Mustang at Bergen Air Show (2005, age 71 — pre-80)
- Awarded 2023 Michael Collins Trophy for Lifetime Achievement by Smithsonian (March 2023, age 89)
- **Continued flying vintage T-34 aircraft until fatal crash at age 90** — was still actively piloting when he died
- Founded William A. Anders Foundation post-retirement
