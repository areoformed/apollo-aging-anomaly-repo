## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy (active duty) and United States Naval Reserve
- **Rank:** Commander, USNR (attained 1969)
- Naval Aviator; received Wings of Gold in 1955 at Naval Air Station Corpus Christi
- Served four years active duty at San Diego and aboard USS Hancock
- Logged over 4,500 flight hours total (4,000 in jet aircraft)

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Physics (1953, University of Utah) — high honors
- Master of Science in Physics (University of California, Berkeley)
- PhD in High-Energy Nuclear Physics (1964, UC Berkeley)
- Dissertation: _Differential Distribution of Charge-Exchange and Inelastic Neutrons in Π−-p Interactions at 313 and 371 MeV_

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Space physicist at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center (1964–1966)
- Research on low-energy particles in Earth's magnetosphere and interplanetary space
- Postdoctoral research at University of Alaska Fairbanks's Geophysical Institute (1975–1976)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected with NASA Astronaut Group 5 ("Original Nineteen") in April 1966
- Helped develop Apollo 11 EVA activities and lunar surface experiments
- CAPCOM for Apollo 11 and Apollo 12
- Backup pilot for Skylab 3 and Skylab 4; on standby for Skylab Rescue mission
- 19-year gap between astronaut selection and spaceflight — longer than any continuously serving American astronaut at that time

---

## Missions Flown
- **STS-51-B** (April 29 – May 6, 1985) — Payload Commander and Lead Mission Specialist
  - Space Shuttle Challenger; Spacelab-3 science mission
  - Duration: 7 days, 0 hours, 8 minutes; 110 Earth orbits

## Post-NASA Career
- Retired from NASA in April 1986
- Professor of Physics and Astronomy at Utah State University (1986–1995)
