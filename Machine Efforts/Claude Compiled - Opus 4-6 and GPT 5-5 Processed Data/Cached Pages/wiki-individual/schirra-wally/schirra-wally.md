# Biographical Data: Wally Schirra

**Full Name:** Walter Marty Schirra Jr.

**Birth:** March 12, 1923, Hackensack, New Jersey

**Death:** May 3, 2007 (age 84), San Diego, California

**Cause of Death:** Heart attack while undergoing treatment for abdominal cancer at Scripps Green Hospital

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- **Service periods:** Commissioned June 6, 1945; retired July 1, 1969
- Korean War service: 90 combat missions as fighter pilot; downed two MiG-15s
- Decorations: Navy Distinguished Service Medal, three Distinguished Flying Crosses, three Air Medals

---

## Education
- Newark College of Engineering (now NJIT), enrolled after high school
- United States Naval Academy, Bachelor of Science degree (1945, completed in 3 years due to wartime acceleration)
- U.S. Naval Test Pilot School (1958, Class 20)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Naval aviator (wings earned 1948)
- Fighter Squadron 71 pilot
- Korean War combat pilot (1950-1951)
- Test pilot at Naval Ordnance Test Station China Lake
- First pilot to fire the Sidewinder missile
- Test pilot assignments with various Navy jet fighters (F7U Cutlass, F3H Demon, F4H Phantom)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as one of Mercury Seven astronauts (April 1959)
- Fifth American and ninth human to travel into space
- First astronaut to fly three times in space
- Only astronaut to fly in Mercury, Gemini, and Apollo programs
- Logged 295 hours 15 minutes total spacetime

**Missions:**
1. **Mercury-Atlas 8** (Sigma 7): October 3, 1962 — six-orbit, nine-hour mission
2. **Gemini VI-A**: December 1965 — achieved first space rendezvous with Gemini 7, maintaining station-keeping within one foot
3. **Apollo 7**: October 11, 1968 — 11-day low Earth orbit shakedown test; commanded first crewed Apollo flight

---

## Post-NASA Career
- CBS News consultant (1969-1975), co-anchored moon landing coverage with Walter Cronkite
- Television spokesperson for Actifed decongestant (based on his Apollo 7 cold)
- President, Regency Investors Incorporated
- President and CEO, Environmental Control Company (1970-1973)
- Consultant work on Alaskan oil pipeline
- Board member advisory for U.S. national parks (1973-1985)
- Founder, Schirra Enterprises (1979); consultant until 1980
- Board positions: Electromedics, Finalco, Kimberly-Clark, Net Air International, Rocky Mountain Airlines, Johns-Manville
- President, Prometheus energy development company (1980-1981)
- Co-founder, Mercury Seven Foundation (1984, later Astronaut Scholarship Foundation)

**Writing Career:**
- Co-author, "We Seven" (1962) — Mercury Seven memoir
- Author, "Schirra's Space" (1988) — autobiography with Richard N. Billings
- Co-author, "Wildcats to Tomcats" (1995) — naval aviation history
- Co-author, "The Real Space Cowboys" (2005)
- Contributor, "In the Shadow of the Moon" (2007)
