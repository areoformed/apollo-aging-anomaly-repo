# Alan Bean: Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Alan LaVern Bean

**Birth:** March 15, 1932, Wheeler, Texas

**Death:** May 26, 2018 (age 86), Houston, Texas

**Cause of Death:** Sudden onset of illness while in Fort Wayne, Indiana two weeks prior

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- Enlisted in Naval Reserve (1949); commissioned ensign (1955); naval aviator wings (1956)
- Fighter pilot in Attack Squadron 44 (1956-1960)
- Test pilot (1960); retired October 1975

---

## Education
- **BS in Aeronautical Engineering:** University of Texas at Austin (1955)
- U.S. Naval Test Pilot School, NAS Patuxent River (graduated November 1960)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Fighter pilot with 7,145+ flight hours (4,890 in jets)
- Test pilot on naval aircraft

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (1963)

**Missions:**
1. **Apollo 12** (November 1969) — Lunar Module Pilot; fourth person to walk on the Moon; 7+ hours lunar surface exploration; deployed first nuclear power generator on Moon
2. **Skylab 3** (July 29 - September 25, 1973) — Commander; 59 days in space; 150% mission goal achievement

**Total Time in Space:** 69 days, 15 hours, 45 minutes
**EVAs:** 3 total; 10 hours 12 minutes
**Retirement from NASA:** June 1981

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Retired from Navy (1975); continued civilian NASA role until 1981
- **Painting career (1981 onward):** Specialized in lunar/space artwork; incorporated Moon dust, suit patches, lunar tools into paintings
- Exhibited at Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum (2009)
- Publications: *My Life As An Astronaut* (1989), *Painting Apollo* (2009), and others

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- Painting career continued through life (active through mid-80s)
- Exhibited artwork at major institutions
- Published *Painting Apollo* at age 77 (2009)
- Maintained artist website and gallery presence
- Died at 86 from sudden illness
