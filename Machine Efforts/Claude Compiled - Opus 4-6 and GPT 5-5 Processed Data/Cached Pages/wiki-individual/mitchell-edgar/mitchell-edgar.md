# Edgar Mitchell

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Mitchell
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Edgar Dean Mitchell

**Date of Birth:** September 17, 1930, Hereford, Texas

**Date of Death:** February 4, 2016 (aged 85), West Palm Beach, Florida (under hospice care)

**Cause of Death:** Not specified in document

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- Commissioned as Ensign in 1953 through Officer Candidate School at Newport, Rhode Island
- Designated Naval Aviator in 1954
- Accumulated 5,000 flight hours (2,000 in jets)
- Retired October 1, 1972

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science (Industrial Management), Carnegie Institute of Technology (1952)
- Bachelor of Science (Aeronautical Engineering), Naval Postgraduate School (1961)
- Doctor of Science (Aeronautics & Astronautics), MIT (1964)
- U.S. Air Force Aerospace Research Pilot School (1965–1966, graduated first in class)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Served as naval aviator and test pilot; instructor in advanced mathematics and navigation theory for astronaut candidates; Chief of Project Management Division for Manned Orbiting Laboratory (1964–1965).

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected NASA Astronaut Group 5 (1966)
- Apollo 13 Mission Operations Team member; awarded Presidential Medal of Freedom (1970)
- Apollo 14 Mission (February 5–6, 1971): Lunar Module Pilot
  - Sixth person to walk on the Moon
  - Nine hours on lunar surface in Fra Mauro Highlands
  - Collected nearly 100 pounds of lunar samples
- Backup Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 16

---

## Missions Flown
- **Apollo 14** (1971) — Lunar Module Pilot

## Time in Space
216 hours 42 minutes; 2 EVAs totaling 9 hours 23 minutes

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Founded Edgar D. Mitchell & Associates (1972)
- Founding chairman of Institute of Noetic Sciences (1973)
- President of Edgar Mitchell Corporation (1974–1978)
- Co-founded Association of Space Explorers (1983)
- Conducted parapsychology research and ESP experiments
- Publicly advocated UFO disclosure theories
- Author: _The Way of the Explorer_ (1996), _Earthrise_ (2014)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- Published _Earthrise: My Adventures as an Apollo 14 Astronaut_ in 2014 (age 83)
- Gave interviews as recently as 2015 discussing extraterrestrial contact claims with the Daily Mirror
- Participated in documentaries and maintained involvement with the Institute of Noetic Sciences throughout the final decade of his life
