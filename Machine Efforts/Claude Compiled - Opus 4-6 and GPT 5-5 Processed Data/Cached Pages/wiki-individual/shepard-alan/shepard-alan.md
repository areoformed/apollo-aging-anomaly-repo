# Alan Shepard - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Alan Bartlett Shepard Jr.

**Date of Birth:** November 18, 1923

**Date of Death:** July 21, 1998 (aged 74)

**Cause of Death:** Complications from chronic lymphocytic leukemia

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Final Rank:** Rear Admiral (promoted August 26, 1971)
- **Service Period:** 1944-1974
- **Key Actions:** Destroyer service (USS Cogswell) in Pacific during WWII; naval aviator from 1947; test pilot from 1950

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science:** United States Naval Academy, Annapolis (graduated June 6, 1944, ranked 463rd of 915)
- **Master of Arts:** Naval War College, Newport, Rhode Island (graduated 1957)
- **Honorary Degrees:** MA from Dartmouth College (1962); D.Sc. from Miami University (1971); Doctorate of Humanities from Franklin Pierce College (1972)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Logged over 3,600 flying hours (1,700 in jets) as naval aviator and test pilot, including carrier qualifications and evaluation of multiple aircraft types (F4U Corsair, F2H Banshee, F3H Demon, F-8 Crusader, others).

---

## NASA Career Highlights

**Selection:** Mercury Seven astronaut group (April 9, 1959)

**Chief Astronaut:** November 1963 to August 1969; June 1971 to April 30, 1974

**Missions:**
1. **Mercury-Redstone 3** (May 5, 1961) - Freedom 7
   - Second person in space; first American
   - Suborbital flight: 15 minutes, reached 101.2 nautical miles altitude
   - Splashdown 263.1 nautical miles downrange

2. **Apollo 14** (January 31 - February 9, 1971)
   - Mission Commander
   - Fifth person to walk on Moon; oldest at age 47
   - Piloted Lunar Module "Antares"
   - Hit two golf balls on lunar surface (24 and 40 yards respectively)

**Time in Space:** 9 days, 0 hours, 16 minutes total

**EVA Activity:** 2 spacewalks; 9 hours 23 minutes total EVA time

---

## Post-NASA Career (1974-1998)
- Founded Seven Fourteen Enterprises, Inc. (umbrella company for business ventures)
- Served on corporate boards
- Made fortune in banking and real estate
- Co-founded Mercury Seven Foundation (1984, renamed Astronaut Scholarship Foundation in 1995); served as first president and chairman until October 1997
- Co-authored book "Moon Shot: The Inside Story of America's Race to the Moon" (1994)
- Appointed UN delegate to 26th General Assembly (September-December 1971)

---

## Notable Medical Events
**Meniere's Disease (1963):** Inner-ear condition caused grounding from flight status; surgically corrected May 14, 1968, restoring full flight status by May 7, 1969.
