# Joseph P. Allen - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Joseph Percival Allen IV

**Date of Birth:** June 27, 1937 (age 88)

**Date of Death:** Not deceased

**Cause of Death:** N/A

---

## Military Service
No military service listed. Allen was a civilian NASA astronaut.

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Arts** (1959): Mathematics and Physics, DePauw University
- **Master of Science** (1961): Physics, Yale University
- **Doctor of Philosophy** (1965): Physics, Yale University
  - Dissertation: "Studies of Odd-A Nuclei in the 2S-1D Shell"
  - Advisor: D. Allan Bromley

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Research associate, Nuclear Physics Laboratory, University of Washington
- Staff physicist, Nuclear Structure Laboratory, Yale University (1965-1966)
- Guest researcher, Brookhaven National Laboratory (1963-1967)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
Selected as scientist-astronaut in August 1967 (NASA Astronaut Group 6). Notable positions:
- Mission scientist, Apollo 15 support crew
- NASA Assistant Administrator for Legislative Affairs (1975-1978)
- CAPCOM during Space Shuttle Columbia reentry (1981)

---

## Missions Flown
1. **STS-5** (November 11-16, 1982): First fully operational Space Shuttle flight
   - Deployed two commercial communications satellites
   - Planned spacewalk was cancelled

2. **STS-51-A** (November 8-16, 1984): Space Shuttle Discovery
   - Deployed two satellites
   - First space salvage attempt: retrieved Palapa B-2 and Westar VI satellites

---

## Time in Space
- **Total:** 314 hours
- **EVA missions:** 2 spacewalks, 11 hours 42 minutes total EVA time

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Chief Executive Officer, Space Industries International
- Chairman, Veridian (until retirement in 2004)
- Consultant for 1998 film "Armageddon" directed by Michael Bay
- Inducted into U.S. Astronaut Hall of Fame (2005)

## Personal Life
Married Bonnie Jo Darling in 1961; two children. His wife passed away in early 2021.
