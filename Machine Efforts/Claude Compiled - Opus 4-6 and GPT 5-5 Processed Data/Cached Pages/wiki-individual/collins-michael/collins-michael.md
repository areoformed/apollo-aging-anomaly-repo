# Michael Collins (Astronaut) - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Michael Collins

**Birth:** October 31, 1930, Rome, Italy

**Death:** April 28, 2021, Naples, Florida (age 90)

**Cause of Death:** Cancer

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Final Rank:** Major General, USAF Reserve
- Active duty: 1952-1970; Reserve: 1970-1982
- Flew F-86 Sabre fighters in France (1954-1957)
- Over 3,000 flying hours (2,700 in jets)

---

## Education
- **BS in Military Science:** United States Military Academy, West Point (1952)
- USAF Experimental Flight Test Pilot School (Class 60C, 1960)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School (Class III, 1963)
- Harvard Business School Advanced Management Program (1974)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Instructor and test pilot at Edwards AFB
- Commander of training detachments at Nellis AFB

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (1963)

**Missions:**
1. **Gemini 10** (July 18-21, 1966) — Pilot; orbital rendezvous with two Agena vehicles; two EVAs; altitude record of 475 miles
2. **Apollo 11** (July 16-24, 1969) — Command Module Pilot; orbited Moon 30 times while Armstrong and Aldrin landed

**Total Time in Space:** 11 days, 2 hours, 4 minutes
**EVAs:** 2 spacewalks, 1 hour 28 minutes total

---

## Post-NASA Career

**Government Service:**
- Assistant Secretary of State for Public Affairs (1970-1971)
- Director, National Air and Space Museum (1971-1978) — secured $40M funding, oversaw construction and opening July 1, 1976
- Undersecretary, Smithsonian Institution (1978+)

**Private Sector:**
- VP, LTV Aerospace (1980-1985)
- Founded Michael Collins Associates consulting firm (1985)

**Writing:**
- *Carrying the Fire* (1974) — regarded as best astronaut autobiography
- *Liftoff* (1988), *Mission to Mars* (1990), *Flying to the Moon* (1976/1994)
- Watercolor painter (Everglades and aircraft subjects)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- Congressional Gold Medal ceremony at U.S. Capitol (November 2011, age 80)
- Attended Neil Armstrong memorial (2012, age 81)
- Photographed with President Trump at Apollo 50th anniversary (July 2019, age 88)
- Featured in documentary *Apollo 11* (2019, age 88)
- Maintained public engagement through late 80s
- Died at 90 from cancer
