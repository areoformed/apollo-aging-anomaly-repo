# John S. Bull

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_S._Bull
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** John Sumter Bull

**Date of Birth:** September 25, 1934

**Date of Death:** August 11, 2008 (aged 73)

**Cause of Death:** Complications related to long-term asthma

**Place of Death:** South Lake Tahoe, California

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Lieutenant Commander
- **Service Period:** June 1957 onwards
- **Flight Experience:** Over 2,100 total flight hours; 1,800 hours in jet aircraft. Flew F-3 Demon and F-4 Phantom II aircraft (March 1959–November 1960) with VF-114 squadron aboard USS Ranger, USS Hancock, and USS Kitty Hawk.

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering, Rice University (1957)
- Master of Science in Aeronautical Engineering, Stanford University (1971)
- Ph.D. in Aeronautical Engineering, Stanford University (1973)
- Dissertation: "Precise Attitude Control of the Stanford Relativity Satellite"

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Graduated from U.S. Naval Test Pilot School (February 1964) as an outstanding graduate. Served as project test pilot in the Carrier Suitability Branch at Naval Air Test Center, Patuxent River, Maryland.

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected: NASA Astronaut Group 5, 1966
- Support crew for Apollo 8 (November 1967)
- Named Lunar Module Pilot for LTA-8 environmental qualification test; replaced due to severe sinus problems (May–June 1968)
- Diagnosed with pulmonary disease; resigned from astronaut corps (July 1968)

---

## Post-Astronaut NASA Work
- NASA Ames Research Center (1973–1985): Conducted simulation and flight test research in advanced flight systems
- Chief, Aircraft Systems Branch (circa 1983)
- Managed NASA-wide autonomous systems research (1986–1989)
- Consultant at Ames (until 1997)
