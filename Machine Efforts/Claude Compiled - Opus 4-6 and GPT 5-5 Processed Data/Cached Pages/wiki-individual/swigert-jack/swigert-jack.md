# Jack Swigert

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Swigert
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** John Leonard Swigert Jr.

**Date of Birth:** August 30, 1931, Denver, Colorado

**Date of Death:** December 27, 1982, Washington, D.C. (aged 51)

**Cause of Death:** Respiratory failure from bone marrow cancer (initially diagnosed with malignant nasal tumor early 1982, then bone marrow cancer August 1982)

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Captain
- **Service:** 1953–1956 (active duty); 1956–1965 (reserve)
- Massachusetts Air National Guard (1957–1960); Connecticut Air National Guard (1960–1965)

---

## Education
- B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, University of Colorado, Boulder (1953)
- M.S. in Aerospace Engineering, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (1965)
- M.B.A., University of Hartford (1967)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Fighter pilot in Japan and South Korea (1953+)
- Engineering test pilot for Pratt & Whitney (1957–1964)
- Test pilot for North American Aviation
- Logged over 7,200 flight hours, including 5,725+ in jet aircraft

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected: NASA Astronaut Group 5 (April 1966)
- Command Module Pilot, Apollo 13 — selected just three days before launch, replacing Ken Mattingly
- Famous announcement: "Houston, we've had a problem here"
- Removed from Apollo-Soyuz Test Project (1972) due to Apollo 15 postal covers incident
- Retired from NASA: August 1977

---

## Missions Flown
- **Apollo 13** (April 11–17, 1970) — Command Module Pilot

## Time in Space
5 days, 22 hours, 54 minutes

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Executive director, House Committee on Science and Astronautics (1973–1977)
- Vice president, B.D.M. Corporation, Golden, Colorado (1979–1981)
- Vice president for financial affairs, International Gold and Minerals Limited (1981–1982)
- Elected to U.S. House representing Colorado's 6th district (November 2, 1982, 64% of vote)
- Died before taking office — seven days before his term would have begun

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
Not applicable — Swigert died at age 51 from cancer.
