# Bruce McCandless II

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_McCandless_II
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Byron Willis McCandless (later Bruce McCandless II)

**Date of Birth:** June 8, 1937, Boston, Massachusetts

**Date of Death:** December 21, 2017, Los Angeles, California (age 80)

**Cause of Death:** Not specified in document

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- **Flight Experience:** Over 5,200 flying hours including 5,000 in jet aircraft
- Designated Naval Aviator March 1960

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science, United States Naval Academy (1958, graduated 2nd in class of 899)
- Master of Science in Electrical Engineering, Stanford University (1965)
- Master of Business Administration, University of Houston–Clear Lake (1987)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Fighter Squadron 102 pilot (1960–1964) aboard USS Forrestal and USS Enterprise; participated in Cuban Missile Crisis operations; instrument flight instructor (early 1964)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected: NASA Astronaut Group 5, April 1966 (age 28, youngest member)
- CAPCOM for Apollo 11 launch and lunar EVA
- Backup pilot for Skylab 2
- Co-investigator on M-509 maneuvering unit experiment
- First untethered spacewalk using Manned Maneuvering Unit (MMU)

---

## Missions Flown
1. **STS-41-B** (February 3–11, 1984) — First untethered spacewalk using MMU; first EVA lasted 6 hours 17 minutes
2. **STS-31** (April 24–29, 1990) — Deployed Hubble Space Telescope from record 380-mile altitude

## Time in Space
312+ hours total; 4 hours MMU flight time; 2 total EVAs; 12 hours 12 minutes total EVA time

---

## Post-NASA Career
Employed by Lockheed Martin Space Systems after retiring in 1990

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- 2010 (age 73): Initiated lawsuit against singer Dido regarding unauthorized use of spacewalk photograph on album artwork (settled January 2011)
- Died at age 80; limited evidence of specific activities in final years
- Son published book _Wonders All Around: The Incredible True Story of Astronaut Bruce McCandless II_ (2021), indicating ongoing public engagement with his legacy
