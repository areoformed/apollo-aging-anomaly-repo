# Donald Herod Peterson - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Donald Herod Peterson

**Date of Birth:** October 22, 1933, Winona, Mississippi

**Date of Death:** May 27, 2018 (aged 84), El Lago, Texas

**Cause of Death:** Alzheimer's disease and bone cancer

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Colonel
- Active service: 24+ years; retired from USAF, continued as NASA civilian astronaut

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** (1955): United States Military Academy (West Point)
- **Master of Science** (1962): Nuclear Engineering, Air Force Institute of Technology/Air University
- Pursued Ph.D. coursework, University of Texas at Austin

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Flight instructor and military training officer, Air Training Command (4 years)
- Nuclear systems analyst, Air Force Systems Command (3 years)
- Fighter pilot, Tactical Air Command (1 year, including 3 months combat weapons training)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School graduate, Edwards AFB
- Over 5,300 flight hours logged; 5,000+ in jet aircraft
- MOL (Manned Orbiting Laboratory) program astronaut selectee, 1967

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected for NASA Astronaut Group 7 (September 1969) — transferred from cancelled MOL program
- Apollo 16 astronaut support crew
- Performed first shuttle EVA with Story Musgrave (STS-6)
- Retired from NASA Astronaut Corps, November 1984

---

## Missions Flown
- **STS-6** (April 4-9, 1983): Mission specialist; Space Shuttle Challenger maiden voyage; performed first shuttle EVA with Story Musgrave; EVA time: 4 hours 17 minutes

---

## Time in Space
120 hours (5 days, 23 minutes)

---

## Post-NASA Career
Consultant in crewed aerospace operations

No specific evidence of activity at age 80+ documented in the Wikipedia article. Peterson died at 84 from Alzheimer's disease and bone cancer.
