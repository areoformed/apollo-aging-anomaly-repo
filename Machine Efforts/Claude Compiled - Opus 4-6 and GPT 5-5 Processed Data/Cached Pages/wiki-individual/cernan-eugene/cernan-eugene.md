# Eugene Cernan - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Eugene Andrew Cernan

**Date of Birth:** March 14, 1934, Chicago, Illinois

**Date of Death:** January 16, 2017, Houston, Texas (age 82)

**Cause of Death:** Not specified on Wikipedia

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- **Service Period:** Commissioned 1956; retired July 1, 1976
- **Flight Hours:** Over 5,000 total; 4,800 in jets; approximately 200 carrier landings

---

## Education
- **BS in Electrical Engineering:** Purdue University (1956)
- **MS in Aeronautical Engineering:** Naval Postgraduate School (1963)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Fighter pilot; flew F9F Panther, FJ-4 Fury, A-4 Skyhawk in Attack Squadrons 126 and 113

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 1963)

**Missions:**
1. **Gemini 9A** (June 1966) — Pilot; performed second American EVA
2. **Apollo 10** (May 18-26, 1969) — Lunar Module Pilot; descended to 8.5 nautical miles above lunar surface
3. **Apollo 17** (December 11-14, 1972) — Commander; final Apollo lunar mission; last person to depart the lunar surface

**Total Time in Space:** 23 days, 14 hours, 15 minutes
**EVAs:** 4 total; 24 hours 11 minutes cumulative

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Executive Vice President, Coral Petroleum Inc. (1976)
- Founded The Cernan Corporation (1981)
- ABC News contributor (1987+): covered Shuttle launches and science segments
- Memoir *The Last Man on the Moon* (1999)
- Documentary appearances: *In the Shadow of the Moon* (2007); *The Last Man on the Moon* (2014)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- Testified before U.S. Congress (2010, age 76 — just under 80)
- Participated in documentary *The Last Man on the Moon* (2014, age 80)
- Gave eulogy at Neil Armstrong's funeral (2012, age 78)
- Died at 82
