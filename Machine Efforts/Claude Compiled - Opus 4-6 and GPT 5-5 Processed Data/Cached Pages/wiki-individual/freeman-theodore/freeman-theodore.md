# Theodore Freeman - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Theodore Cordy Freeman

**Birth:** February 18, 1930, Haverford, Pennsylvania

**Death:** October 31, 1964 (aged 34), Houston, Texas

**Cause of Death:** T-38A Talon jet crash during approach to Ellington AFB; bird strike (goose) caused engine flame-out; parachute did not deploy in time after ejection

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Captain (O-3)
- Pilot wings: February 1955
- Last assignment: Flight test engineer and instructor at Aerospace Research Pilot School, Edwards AFB

---

## Education
- University of Delaware (1948-1949)
- **BS:** United States Naval Academy (1953)
- **MS in Aeronautical Engineering:** University of Michigan (1960)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Air Force pilot and test pilot; 3,300+ flight hours (2,400+ in jets)
- Graduated Air Force Experimental Flight Test Pilot School (Class 62A)
- Graduated Aerospace Research Pilot School (Class IV)

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 1963)
**Assignment:** Booster development
**First fatality in NASA Astronaut Corps**

**Missions Flown:** None — killed in training accident

Not applicable — died at age 34.
