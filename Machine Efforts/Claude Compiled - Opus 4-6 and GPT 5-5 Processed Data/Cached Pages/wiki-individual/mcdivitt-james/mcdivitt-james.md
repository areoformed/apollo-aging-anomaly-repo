# James McDivitt - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** James Alton McDivitt Jr.

**Birth:** June 10, 1929, Chicago, Illinois

**Death:** October 13, 2022 (age 93), Tucson, Arizona

**Cause of Death:** Died in his sleep

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force (USAF)
- **Rank:** Brigadier General (promoted February 17, 1972)
- **Service Period:** January 1951 - June 1972
- **Combat History:** 145 combat missions during Korean War in F-80 Shooting Stars and F-86 Sabres
- **Awards:** 4 Distinguished Flying Crosses, 2 Air Force Distinguished Service Medals, 5 Air Medals

---

## Education
- Jackson Junior College (1948-1950)
- **University of Michigan:** B.S. in Aeronautical Engineering (1959, first in class)
- Air Force Experimental Flight Test Pilot School (Class 59C)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School (Class I)
- Honorary doctorates: University of Michigan (1965), Seton Hall (1969), Miami University Ohio (1970), Eastern Michigan (1975)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Pilot and assistant operations officer, 19th Fighter Interceptor Squadron, Dow AFB
- Pilot and flight commander, 332d Fighter Interceptor Squadron, McGuire AFB
- Experimental test pilot at Edwards Air Force Base (June 1959 onward)
- Over 2,500 flight hours by 1962 (2,000+ in jets)
- Chase pilot for Robert M. White's X-15 flight (July 17, 1962)

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 2, September 1962

**Missions:**
1. **Gemini 4** (June 3-7, 1965) — Command Pilot; 97 hours 56 minutes, 62 orbits; Ed White performed first U.S. spacewalk during this mission
2. **Apollo 9** (March 3-13, 1969) — Commander; first crewed test of Lunar Module; 151 orbits

**Total Time in Space:** 14 days, 2 hours, 56 minutes

**Management Roles:**
- Manager of Lunar Landing Operations (May 1969)
- Apollo Spacecraft Program Manager (August 1969-June 1972)

---

## Post-NASA Career (Left NASA/USAF June 1972)
- Executive Vice President, Consumers Power Company
- Executive Vice President, Pullman, Inc.
- President, Pullman Standard Division
- Senior Vice President Government Operations, Rockwell International (retired 1995)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- Participated in 50th anniversary of Apollo 9 (March 2019, age 89), appearing in photographs with Scott and Schweickart
- Maintained professional memberships including Society of Experimental Test Pilots and AIAA
- Lived to age 93; died in his sleep
