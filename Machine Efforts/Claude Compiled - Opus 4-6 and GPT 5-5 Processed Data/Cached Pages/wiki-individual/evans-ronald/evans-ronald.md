# Ronald Evans

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Evans_(astronaut)
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Ronald Ellwin Evans Jr.

**Date of Birth:** November 10, 1933, St. Francis, Kansas

**Date of Death:** April 6, 1990, Scottsdale, Arizona (aged 56)

**Cause of Death:** Heart attack occurring in his sleep at his home

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Final Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- **Service Period:** 1956–1976 (21 years total)
- Naval aviator designated 1957
- Fighter pilot with VF-142 and VF-51
- Combat missions during Vietnam War (112 total sorties)

---

## Education
- B.S. in Electrical Engineering, University of Kansas (1956)
- M.S. in Aeronautical Engineering, Naval Postgraduate School, Monterey, California (1964)
- Russian language coursework at Defense Language Institute

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Served as naval aviator and fighter pilot, flying the Vought F8U Crusader and other aircraft across multiple squadrons and deployments to the Western Pacific theater.

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected: NASA Astronaut Group 5 (April 1966)
- Support crew: Apollo 1, Apollo 7, Apollo 11
- CAPCOM: Apollo 7, 11, 14
- Backup Command Module Pilot: Apollo 14
- Prime Command Module Pilot: Apollo 17

---

## Missions Flown
- **Apollo 17** (December 1972) — Command Module Pilot
  - Duration: 301 hours, 51 minutes, 59 seconds
  - Lunar Orbit Record: 147 hours, 43 minutes — most time ever spent orbiting the Moon
  - 1 EVA: 1 hour, 5 minutes, 44 seconds (final spacewalk of Apollo program)
- Backup Command Module Pilot for Apollo-Soyuz Test Project (1975)

## Time in Space
301 hours, 51 minutes, 59 seconds

---

## Post-NASA Career (departed March 8, 1977)
- Director of Marketing, Western America Energy Corporation (Scottsdale, Arizona)
- Director of Space Systems Marketing, Sperry Flight Systems
- Founder of consulting company with partnership developing Japanese space-themed theme park

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
Not applicable — Evans died at age 56 in 1990 of a heart attack.
