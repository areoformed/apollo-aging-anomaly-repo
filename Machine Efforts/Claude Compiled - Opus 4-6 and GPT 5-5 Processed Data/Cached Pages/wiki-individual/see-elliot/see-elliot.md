# Elliot See - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Elliot McKay See Jr.

**Birth:** July 23, 1927, Dallas, Texas

**Death:** February 28, 1966 (age 38), St. Louis, Missouri

**Cause of Death:** Trauma sustained in T-38 jet aircraft crash at McDonnell Aircraft Building 101

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy Reserve
- **Rank:** Commander
- Called to active duty during Korean War; flew F9F Panther fighters with Fighter Squadron 144

---

## Education
- University of Texas, Austin (attended)
- United States Merchant Marine Academy (BS in Marine Engineering, 1949)
- University of California, Los Angeles (MS in Aeronautical Engineering, 1962)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- General Electric (1949-1953, 1956-1962): Aircraft Gas Turbine Division engineer; flight test engineer; experimental test pilot at Edwards AFB
- Flew F-86, F-104, F11F-1F Super Tiger, RB-66, F4H, T-38; logged 3,900+ flight hours (3,300+ in jets)

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 2, 1962

**Assignments:**
- Backup pilot for Gemini 5 (1965)
- Prime command pilot for Gemini 9 (assigned October 1965)

**Missions Flown:** None — killed in T-38 crash before spaceflight

---

Not applicable — died at age 38.
