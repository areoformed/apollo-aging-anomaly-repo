# Franklin Story Musgrave - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Franklin Story Musgrave

**Date of Birth:** August 19, 1935 (currently age 90)

**Date of Death:** N/A (living)

**Cause of Death:** N/A

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Marine Corps
- **Enlisted:** 1953
- **Roles:** Aviation electrician, instrument technician, aircraft crew chief
- **Served in:** Korea, Japan, Hawaii, and aboard USS Wasp (CV-18)
- Also served as United States Air Force postdoctoral fellow (1965-1966)

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** (1958): Mathematics and Statistics, Syracuse University
- **Master of Business Administration** (1959): Operations Analysis and Computer Programming, UCLA
- **Bachelor of Arts** (1960): Chemistry, Marietta College
- **Doctor of Medicine** (1964): Columbia University College of Physicians and Surgeons
- **Master of Science** (1966): Physiology and Biophysics, University of Kentucky
- **Master of Arts** (1987): Literature, University of Houston-Clear Lake

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Mathematician and operations analyst at Eastman Kodak Company (1958)
- Surgical internship at University of Kentucky Medical Center (1964-1965)
- Air Force postdoctoral fellow in aerospace medicine and physiology (1965-1966)
- National Heart Institute postdoctoral fellow (1966-1967)
- Part-time clinical medicine practice at Denver General Hospital (1967-1989)
- Adjunct instructor of physiology and biophysics at University of Kentucky Medical Center

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, NASA Astronaut Group 6 (August 1967)
- Backup Science Pilot for Skylab 2 (1973)
- Participated in design of Space Shuttle extravehicular activity equipment
- Test and verification pilot in Shuttle Avionics Integration Laboratory (1979-1982, 1983-1984)
- CAPCOM for multiple Space Shuttle missions
- First astronaut to conduct spacewalk from Space Shuttle
- Performed 3 of 5 spacewalks during Hubble servicing mission (STS-61)

---

## Missions Flown
1. **STS-6** (April 4-9, 1983): Space Shuttle Challenger
2. **STS-51-F/Spacelab-2** (July 29-August 6, 1985): Space Shuttle Challenger
3. **STS-33** (November 22-27, 1989): Space Shuttle Discovery
4. **STS-44** (November 24-December 1, 1991): Space Shuttle Atlantis
5. **STS-61** (December 2-13, 1993): Space Shuttle Endeavour (Hubble Servicing Mission)
6. **STS-80** (November 19-December 7, 1996): Space Shuttle Columbia

## Notable
Musgrave holds six academic degrees and flew six Space Shuttle missions — the only astronaut to fly on all five Space Shuttles. He is one of the most decorated scientist-astronauts in NASA history.
