# Anthony W. England - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Anthony Wayne England

**Date of Birth:** May 15, 1942 (age 82 at time of article)

**Date of Death:** Not deceased

**Cause of Death:** N/A

---

## Military Service
None listed; received flight training at Laughlin Air Force Base, Texas, earning "wings with distinguished honors in April 1969"

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** (1965): Earth and Planetary Sciences, MIT
- **Master of Science** (1965): Earth and Planetary Sciences, MIT
- **Doctor of Philosophy** (1970): Earth and Planetary Sciences, MIT

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Graduate fellow at MIT (three years before NASA assignment)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, NASA Astronaut Group 6 (August 1967); "the youngest candidate to be selected up to that time" at 25 years, 81 days
- Support crewman for Apollo 13 and Apollo 16
- EVA CapCom during Apollo 16 lunar surface operations
- Developed lithium hydroxide canister instructions during Apollo 13 crisis
- Resigned from NASA in 1972 with Philip Chapman
- Returned to Johnson Space Center in 1979 as senior scientist-astronaut

---

## Missions Flown
- **STS-51-F** (July 29 - August 6, 1985): Mission specialist aboard Space Shuttle Challenger

---

## Time in Space
188 hours (7 days, 22 hours, 45 minutes)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Deputy chief, Office of Geochemistry and Geophysics (United States Geological Survey)
- Associate editor, Journal of Geophysical Research
- Dean, College of Engineering and Computer Science (University of Michigan-Dearborn)
- Professor of electrical engineering and computer science at University of Michigan, Ann Arbor
- Member, Michigan Pipeline Safety Advisory Board and Mackinac Straits Corridor Authority
