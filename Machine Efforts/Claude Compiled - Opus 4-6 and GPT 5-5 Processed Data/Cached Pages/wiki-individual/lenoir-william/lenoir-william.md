# William Benjamin Lenoir - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** William Benjamin Lenoir

**Date of Birth:** March 14, 1939, Miami, Florida

**Date of Death:** August 26, 2010 (aged 71), Sandoval County, New Mexico

**Cause of Death:** Head injuries sustained in a bicycle accident near his home

---

## Military Service
No military service documented; civilian NASA astronaut

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** (1961): Electrical Engineering, MIT
- **Master of Science** (1962): Electrical Engineering, MIT
- **Doctor of Philosophy** (1965): Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, MIT
  - Thesis: "Remote Sounding of the Upper Atmosphere by Microwave Measurements"

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- MIT instructor (1964-1965)
- MIT Associate Professor of Electrical Engineering (1965 onward)
- Research focused on electromagnetic theory, systems theory, and remote sensing
- Investigated satellite experiments while pursuing doctoral studies

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, August 1967 (NASA Group 6)
- Completed flight training at Laughlin Air Force Base, Texas; received wings with distinguished honors, April 1969
- Backup science pilot for Skylab 3 and Skylab 4
- Leader of NASA Satellite Power Team (September 1974-July 1976)
- Directed mission development within the Astronaut Office post-STS-5
- Associate Administrator for Space Flight (June 1989-April 1992)

---

## Missions Flown
- **STS-5** (November 11-16, 1982): First operational Space Shuttle mission; mission specialist; deployed two commercial communications satellites

---

## Time in Space
5 days, 2 hours, 14 minutes (over 122 hours)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Booz Allen Hamilton, management consultant (September 1984-June 1989; April 1992 onward)
- Vice President, Applied Systems Division, Booz Allen Hamilton, Bethesda, Maryland (1992)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
Not applicable — Lenoir died at age 71 in a bicycle accident. He did not reach age 80.
