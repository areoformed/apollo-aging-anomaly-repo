# Robert Allan Ridley Parker - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Robert Allan Ridley Parker

**Date of Birth:** December 14, 1936

**Date of Death:** Not deceased (age 89)

**Cause of Death:** N/A

## Military Service
No military service documented

---

## Education
- **B.A.** (1958): Astronomy and Physics, Amherst College
- **M.S.** (1962): Astronomy, California Institute of Technology
- **Ph.D.** (1962): Astronomy, California Institute of Technology
  - Dissertation: "The Physical Conditions Pertaining to Some Possible Supernova Remnants"
  - Doctoral advisor: Guido Munch

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Associate professor of astronomy at University of Wisconsin-Madison prior to astronaut selection

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, August 1967 (NASA Group 6)
- Support crew member for Apollo 15 and Apollo 17
- Program scientist for Skylab Program
- Director, Space Flight/Space Station Integration Office (1988-1989)
- Director, Division of Policy and Plans for Office of Space Flight (1991)

---

## Missions Flown
1. **STS-9/Spacelab-1** (November 28-December 8, 1983)
2. **STS-35** (December 2-10, 1990)

19 days, 6 hours, 52 minutes (463 hours total)

## Post-NASA Career
- Director of NASA Management Office at Jet Propulsion Laboratory (1997)
- Retired August 31, 2005
