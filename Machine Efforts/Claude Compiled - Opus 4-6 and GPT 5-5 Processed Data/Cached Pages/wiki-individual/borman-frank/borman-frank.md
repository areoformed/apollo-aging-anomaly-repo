# Frank Borman: Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Frank Frederick Borman II

**Birth:** March 14, 1928, Gary, Indiana

**Death:** November 7, 2023 (age 95), Billings, Montana

**Cause of Death:** Stroke at Billings Clinic

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force (USAF)
- **Rank:** Colonel
- **Service Period:** Commissioned June 2, 1950; retired July 1, 1970
- **Flying Hours:** 3,600 total (3,000 in jet aircraft)

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science:** United States Military Academy (West Point), Class of 1950, ranked 8th of 670
- **Master of Science:** California Institute of Technology, 1957 (aeronautical engineering)
- **Additional Training:** USAF Experimental Flight Test Pilot School (1960-1961); Aerospace Research Pilot School (first class, 1961)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Fighter pilot and flight instructor, multiple Air Force bases (1950-1956)
- Assistant professor of thermodynamics and fluid mechanics, West Point (1957-1960)
- Test pilot, Edwards Air Force Base (1960-1962)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- **Selection:** NASA Group 2 ("Next Nine"), September 17, 1962
- **Time in Space:** 19 days, 21 hours, 35 minutes

**Missions:**
1. **Gemini 7** (December 1965) — Set 14-day spaceflight endurance record as commander
2. **Apollo 8** (December 1968) — Commanded first crewed lunar orbit mission; crew took iconic "Earthrise" photograph

**Other roles:**
- Served on Apollo 1 fire investigation review board
- NASA liaison at White House during Apollo 11 mission (July 1969)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- **Eastern Air Lines (1970-1986):**
  - Senior VP for operations (December 1970)
  - CEO (December 1975), Chairman (December 1976)
  - Produced record $67.3 million profit (1978); resigned June 1986

- **Private Business (1986 onward):**
  - Ford dealership ownership, Las Cruces, New Mexico (with son Fred)
  - Cattle ranch, Bighorn Mountains, Montana (4,000 head on 160,000 acres, purchased 1998)
  - CEO of Patlex Corporation (July 1988-August 1996)
  - Corporate boards: Home Depot, National Geographic, Outboard Marine Corporation, American Superconductor

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+

**Speeches and Public Appearances:**
- Commencement address, University of Arizona (2008, age 80)
- NASA TV appearance discussing Apollo 8 (November 13, 2008)
- Apollo 8 50th anniversary reunion with crewmates (December 2018, age 90)

**Active Hobbies:**
- Rebuilt rare WWII Bell P-63 Kingcobra fighter aircraft; won Grand Champion Warbird at Oshkosh (1998)
- Personally flew restored aircraft in airshows
- Active in Society of Antique Modelers and model airplane community

**Personal:**
- Daily visits to wife Susan in nursing home until her death (September 7, 2021)
- Became oldest living American astronaut after John Glenn's death (December 2016)
- Celebrated 90th birthday March 2018
- Lived to age 95

---

## Awards & Honors
Congressional Space Medal of Honor (1978); NASA Distinguished Service Medal; Harmon Trophy (1965, 1968); Robert J. Collier Trophy; International Space Hall of Fame (1982); U.S. Astronaut Hall of Fame (1993)
