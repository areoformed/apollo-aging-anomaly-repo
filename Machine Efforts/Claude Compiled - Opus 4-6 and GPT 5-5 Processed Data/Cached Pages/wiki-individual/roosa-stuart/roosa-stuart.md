# Stuart Roosa

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_Roosa
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Stuart Allen Roosa

**Date of Birth:** August 16, 1933, Durango, Colorado

**Date of Death:** December 12, 1994, Falls Church, Virginia (aged 61)

**Cause of Death:** Complications from pancreatitis

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Colonel
- Fighter pilot at Langley Air Force Base (F-84F Thunderstreak and F-100 Super Sabre)
- Maintenance flight test pilot at Olmstead Air Force Base (F-101 Voodoo)
- Experimental test pilot at Edwards Air Force Base
- Aerospace Research Pilot School graduate (Class 64C)
- Retired: February 1, 1976

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Aeronautical Engineering (honors), University of Colorado Boulder (1960)
- Also attended Oklahoma State University and University of Arizona
- Harvard Business School Advanced Management Program (1973)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Career began as a smokejumper with the U.S. Forest Service during the 1953 fire season in Oregon and California. Completed Aviation Cadet Program at Williams Air Force Base, Arizona.

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as part of NASA Astronaut Group 5 (1966)
- Capsule Communicator during the Apollo 1 fire (January 27, 1967)
- Support crew member for Apollo 9 (1969)
- Backup Command Module Pilot for Apollo 16 and Apollo 17

---

## Missions Flown
- **Apollo 14** (January 31 – February 9, 1971) — Command Module Pilot
  - Orbited the Moon 34 times; 33 hours in solo lunar orbit
  - Carried tree seeds ("Moon Trees") as part of U.S. Forest Service/NASA project

## Time in Space
9 days, 1 minute (217 hours); 5,500 total flight hours (5,000 in jets)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Founded Gulf Coast Coors in 1981; served as president until his death
- Held various positions in international and U.S. businesses
- Memberships: Society of Experimental Test Pilots, Explorers Club

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
Not applicable — Roosa died at age 61.
