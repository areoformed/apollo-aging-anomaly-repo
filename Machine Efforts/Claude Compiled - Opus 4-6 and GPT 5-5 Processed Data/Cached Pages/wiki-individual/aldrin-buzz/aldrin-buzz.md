# Buzz Aldrin - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Edwin Eugene Aldrin Jr. (legally changed first name to "Buzz" in 1988)

**Date of Birth:** January 20, 1930

**Date of Death:** Not deceased (age 96 as of 2026)

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Brigadier General (honorary promotion May 5, 2023)
- **Service Duration:** 21 years (1951-1972)
- **Combat:** 66 missions in Korean War; shot down two MiG-15 aircraft

---

## Education
- **BS in Mechanical Engineering:** United States Military Academy (1951)
- **MS in Astronautics:** Massachusetts Institute of Technology (1962)
- **ScD in Astronautics:** MIT (January 1963)
- Dissertation: "Line-of-Sight Guidance Techniques for Manned Orbital Rendezvous"

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Fighter pilot (F-80, F-86 Sabre, F-100 Super Sabre)
- Aerial gunnery instructor at Nellis AFB
- Doctoral researcher at MIT (1959-1963)
- Gemini Target Office, Air Force Space Systems Division

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 18, 1963)
- First astronaut with doctoral degree; nicknamed "Dr. Rendezvous"

**Missions:**
1. **Gemini 12** (November 11-15, 1966) — Pilot; three EVAs totaling 5+ hours; resolved EVA fatigue issues
2. **Apollo 11** (July 16-24, 1969) — Lunar Module Pilot; second person to walk on the Moon; "Magnificent desolation"

**Total Time in Space:** 12 days, 1 hour, 53 minutes
**EVA time:** 7 hours, 52 minutes across four spacewalks

**NASA Departure:** July 1, 1971

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Commandant, USAF Aerospace Research Pilot School (1971-1972)
- Retired from USAF: March 1, 1972
- Autobiographies: *Return to Earth* (1973), *Magnificent Desolation* (2009)
- University of North Dakota: College of Aerospace Sciences (1985); helped develop Space Studies program
- Founded Starcraft Boosters, Inc. (1996) for reusable rocket design
- Proposed the Aldrin Cycler for Mars travel (1985)
- Chancellor, International Space University (appointed 2015)
- Mars Society steering committee member

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+

**Age 86 (December 2016):** Visited South Pole Station, becoming oldest person to reach the South Pole

**Age 87 (2017):** Flew with USAF Thunderbirds, becoming oldest person to fly with the team

**Age 89 (2019):** Appeared at State of the Union Address

**Age 93 (January 20, 2023):** Married fourth wife Anca Faur on his birthday

**Age 94 (2024):** Endorsed presidential candidate, citing space exploration policy

**Still living at age 96 as of 2026.** Continued public speaking and advocacy through his 90s.
