# Karl Gordon Henize - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Karl Gordon Henize

**Date of Birth:** October 17, 1926

**Date of Death:** October 5, 1993 (age 66)

**Cause of Death:** High altitude pulmonary edema (HAPE) while on Mount Everest expedition

---

## Military Service
**Branch:** United States Naval Reserve
**Rank:** Lieutenant Commander

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Arts** (1947): Mathematics, University of Virginia
- **Master of Arts** (1948): Astronomy, University of Virginia
- **Doctor of Philosophy** (1954): Astronomy, University of Michigan

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Observer, University of Michigan Observatory at Lamont-Hussey Observatory, South Africa (1948-1951)
- Carnegie postdoctoral fellow, Mount Wilson Observatory (1954)
- Senior astronomer, Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory (1956-1959)
- Associate professor, Northwestern University Department of Astronomy (1959); promoted to full professor (1964)
- Guest observer, Mount Stromlo Observatory, Australia (1961-1962)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, NASA Group 6 (August 1967)
- Support crew member, Apollo 15; CAPCOM
- Support crew member, Skylab 2, 3, and 4 missions
- Awarded NASA Exceptional Scientific Achievement Medal (1974)

---

## Missions Flown
- **STS-51-F** (July 29 - August 6, 1985): Spacelab-2, mission specialist aboard Space Shuttle Challenger
  - 126 orbits

---

## Time in Space
190 hours (7 days, 22 hours, 45 minutes)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Retired as astronaut (April 1986)
- Senior scientist, Space Sciences Branch, NASA
- Studied space debris and space station hazards
- Continued astronomical research and publications (75 papers total; discovered over 2,000 stars)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
Not applicable — Henize died at age 66 during an active mountaineering expedition on Mount Everest, before reaching advanced age. Notably, he was still physically vigorous enough to attempt Everest at 66.
