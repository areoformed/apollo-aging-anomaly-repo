## Post-NASA Career
- Rice University faculty (July 1963 onwards)
- Department chair, Space Physics & Astronomy (1974–1979)
- Andrew Hays Buchanan Professor of Astrophysics (1974–2000)
- Guggenheim Fellow, École Polytechnique, France (1979–1980)
- Humboldt Prize recipient, Max Planck Institute, West Germany (1983–1984)
- Visiting professor, University of Nagoya, Japan (2001–2002)
- Retired officially in 2000 but "continued to be active in research until his death"
