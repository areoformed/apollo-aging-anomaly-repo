# Charles Arthur Bassett II - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Charles Arthur Bassett II

**Birth:** December 30, 1931, Dayton, Ohio

**Death:** February 28, 1966 (aged 34), St. Louis, Missouri

**Cause of Death:** Trauma sustained in T-38 aircraft crash (same accident that killed Elliot See)

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Major (at time of death)
- Over 3,600 flight hours including 2,900+ in jets

---

## Education
- Ohio State University (2 years, 1950-1952)
- Texas Tech University (BS in Electrical Engineering, 1958-1960)
- University of Southern California (graduate work)
- Air Force Experimental Flight Test Pilot School (Class 62A)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School (Class III)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Fighter pilot: flew F-86 Sabre in Korea (1954-1955)
- Test pilot at Edwards Air Force Base

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 1963)

**Assignment:** Pilot for Gemini 9 mission (with Elliot See as command pilot)

**Missions Flown:** None — killed in T-38 crash before first spaceflight

---

## Post-NASA Career
Not applicable — died at age 34.
