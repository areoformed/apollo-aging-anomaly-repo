- **Astronaut Group:** 4 (The Scientists, 1965)

---

**Full Name:** Edward George Gibson

**Date of Birth:** November 8, 1936 (age 89)

**Date of Death:** Not deceased

**Cause of Death:** N/A

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Completed flight training at Williams Air Force Base, Arizona; earned Air Force wings

---

## Education
- **Bachelor of Science** in Engineering, University of Rochester (June 1959)
- **Master of Science** in Engineering (Jet Propulsion), California Institute of Technology (June 1960)
- **PhD** in Engineering with minor in Physics, California Institute of Technology (June 1964)
- **Thesis:** "Ionization Phenomena in a Gas-Particle Plasma"

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Research assistant in jet propulsion at Caltech; senior research scientist at Philco Corporation's Applied Research Laboratories, Newport Beach, California (June 1964 onwards), conducting research in lasers and optical breakdown of gases. Logged over 4,300 flying hours, including 2,270 in jet aircraft.

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, June 1965 (NASA Astronaut Group 4)
- Support crew and CAPCOM for Apollo 12 lunar landing
- Participated in Skylab space station design and testing

---

## Missions Flown
**Skylab 4** (Science Pilot)
- Launch: November 16, 1973
- Landing: February 8, 1974
- Duration: 84 days, 1 hour, 15 minutes
- Completed: 56 experiments, 26 science demonstrations, 15 subsystem objectives, 13 student investigations
- 1,214 Earth revolutions
- 338 hours operating Apollo Telescope Mount for solar observations
- 3 EVAs totaling 15 hours, 22 minutes

---

## Time in Space
**Total:** 84 days, 1 hour, 15 minutes (world record until Soviet Soyuz 26 in March 1978)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Resigned December 1974; worked at Aerospace Corporation on Skylab solar physics data
- March 1976–March 1977: Consultant to ERNO Raumfahrttechnik GmbH, West Germany (Spacelab design)
- March 1977: Chief of Scientist-Astronaut Candidates selection at NASA
- CAPCOM for STS-1
- October 1982: Final NASA retirement
- July 1980–August 1987: Project manager at TRW Space Park (Space Station Freedom support)
- October 1990: Founded Gibson International Corp (consulting on program management, space infrastructure)
- Author of textbook _The Quiet Sun_ (1973; Russian edition 1977); novels _Reach_ (1989) and _In the Wrong Hands_ (1992); edited _The Greatest Adventure_ (1994); recently published _Space: Ever Farther, Ever Faster_ (2025)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- Published book _Space: Ever Farther, Ever Faster_ in 2025 (age 88)
- Maintains consulting firm Gibson International Corp
- Listed as "Motivational Speaker" with active professional presence
- Last surviving Skylab 4 crew member (Carr died 2020; Pogue died 2014)
