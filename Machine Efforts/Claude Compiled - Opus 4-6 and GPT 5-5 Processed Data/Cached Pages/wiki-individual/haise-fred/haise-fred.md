# Fred Haise

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Haise
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 5 (1966)

---

**Full Name:** Fred Wallace Haise Jr.

**Date of Birth:** November 14, 1933 (age 92)

**Date of Death:** Not deceased

---

## Military Service
- U.S. Marine Corps: Captain (1954–1957)
- U.S. Air Force: Captain (1957–1963)

---

## Education
- Perkinston Junior College: AA (1952)
- University of Oklahoma: BS in Aeronautical Engineering (1959)
- U.S. Air Force Aerospace Research Pilot School: Class 64A (1964)
- Harvard Business School: Advanced Management Program (1972)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Research pilot at Lewis Research Center; tactical fighter pilot and chief of standardization-evaluation flight at various Air Force bases; accumulated 9,300 flight hours (6,200 in jets)

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected for NASA Astronaut Group 5 (1966)
- Backup Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 8 and Apollo 11
- First in his astronaut group assigned to a mission

---

## Missions Flown
- **Apollo 13** (1970) — Lunar Module Pilot
- **Approach and Landing Tests** (1977) — Five Space Shuttle Enterprise flights as commander

## Time in Space
5 days, 22 hours, 54 minutes

---

## Post-NASA Career
Left NASA June 1979; test pilot and executive at Grumman Aerospace Corporation (1979–1996)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- Published autobiography "Never Panic Early" (2022, age 88)
- Statue unveiled in Biloxi with handprints in concrete (February 2022)
- Inducted into National Aviation Hall of Fame (September 2023, age 89)
