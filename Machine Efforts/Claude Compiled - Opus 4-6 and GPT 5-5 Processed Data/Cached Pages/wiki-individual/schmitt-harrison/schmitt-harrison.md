# Harrison Schmitt

- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison_Schmitt
- **Cached date:** 2026-05-18
- **Astronaut Group:** 4 (The Scientists, 1965)

---

**Full Name:** Harrison Hagan "Jack" Schmitt

**Date of Birth:** July 3, 1935 (age 90)

**Date of Death:** Not deceased

---

## Military Service
- U.S. Air Force — completed Undergraduate Pilot Training to earn jet pilot qualification as part of astronaut training

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Geology, California Institute of Technology (1957)
- Year of study at University of Oslo, Norway as Fulbright Scholar
- Master of Science and Ph.D. in Geology, Harvard University (1964)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Worked at U.S. Geological Survey's Astrogeology Center in Flagstaff, Arizona, developing geological field techniques for Apollo crews

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as member of NASA Astronaut Group 4 (first scientist-astronauts) in June 1965
- Key role training Apollo crews in lunar geology and sample collection
- First scientist-astronaut to fly in space (December 1972)
- Only professional scientist to land on the Moon

---

## Missions Flown
- **Apollo 17** (December 1972) as Lunar Module Pilot

## Time in Space
12 days, 13 hours, 52 minutes total; 22 hours 3 minutes in extravehicular activity (3 moonwalks)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- U.S. Senator from New Mexico (1977–1983)
- Adjunct professor of engineering physics, University of Wisconsin–Madison
- NASA Advisory Council chair (2005–2008)
- Consultant in geology, space policy, and business
- Author of _Return to the Moon_ (2006)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at 80+
- Appeared in 2009 BBC interview with James May
- Attended 2017 Space Policy Directive signing with President Trump (age 82)
- Gave 2022 interview for Czech television program "Hyde Park Civilization" (age 87)
- Maintains active professional positions including senior research scientist role at Florida Institute for Human & Machine Cognition
