**Full Name:** Jack Robert Lousma

**Date of Birth:** February 29, 1936 (age 90)

**Date of Death:** Not applicable (living)

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Marine Corps
- **Rank:** Colonel
- Aviator wings received: 1960
- Service assignments included VMA-224, VMCJ-2, and Marine Corps Air Stations in Japan and North Carolina
- Retired: November 1, 1983

---

## Education
- Bachelor of Science in Aeronautical Engineering, University of Michigan (1959)
- Master of Science in Aeronautical Engineering, Naval Postgraduate School (1965)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Attack and reconnaissance pilot with the Marine Corps; logged 7,000 flight hours including 4,500 in jets and 240 in helicopters before NASA assignment.

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected in NASA Astronaut Group 5 (April 1966)
- Support crews for Apollo 9, 10, and 13
- CAPCOM during Apollo 13 crisis
- Logged 1,619 hours in space

---

## Missions Flown
1. **Skylab 3** (July 28 – September 25, 1973) — Pilot; 59.5-day mission
2. **STS-3** (March 22–30, 1982) — Commander; 8-day Space Shuttle Columbia mission

## Time in Space
67 days, 11 hours, 13 minutes; 2 spacewalks totaling 11 hours, 1 minute

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Left NASA October 1, 1983
- Republican senatorial candidate in Michigan (1984), losing to incumbent Carl Levin (47.16% to 51.77%)
- Relocated to Texas in September 2013
