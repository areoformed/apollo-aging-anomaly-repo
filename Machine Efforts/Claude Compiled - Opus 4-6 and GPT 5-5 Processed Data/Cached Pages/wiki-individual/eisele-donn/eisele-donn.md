# Donn F. Eisele - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Donn Fulton Eisele

**Birth:** June 23, 1930, Columbus, Ohio

**Death:** December 1, 1987 (age 57), Tokyo, Japan

**Cause of Death:** Heart attack

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Colonel
- Commissioned after Naval Academy graduation (1952)

---

## Education
- **BS:** United States Naval Academy (1952)
- **MS in Astronautics:** Air Force Institute of Technology, Wright-Patterson AFB (1960)
- USAF Experimental Flight Test Pilot School, Edwards AFB (Class 62A, 1962)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Interceptor pilot in South Dakota and Libya (1954-1958)
- Project engineer and experimental test pilot at Air Force Special Weapons Center, Kirtland AFB
- Over 4,200 flight hours (3,600 in jets)

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 1963)

**Mission:**
- **Apollo 7** (October 11-22, 1968) — Command Module Pilot; first crewed Apollo flight test; acted as navigator with star sightings

**Time in Space:** 260 hours (10 days, 20 hours, 8 minutes)
**Backup Role:** CMP for Apollo 10 (1969)
**Resigned from NASA:** 1970
**Final NASA position:** Technical assistant at Langley Research Center (1970-1972)

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Peace Corps Country Director for Thailand (1972-1974)
- Sales Manager, Marion Power Shovel Company/Dresser Industries (1974+)
- Investment accounts manager, Oppenheimer & Company
- Wilton Manors City Commission, Florida (1981)
- Guide for 1986 Concorde Comet Chase flights

---

**Not applicable** — Eisele died at age 57 of a heart attack.
