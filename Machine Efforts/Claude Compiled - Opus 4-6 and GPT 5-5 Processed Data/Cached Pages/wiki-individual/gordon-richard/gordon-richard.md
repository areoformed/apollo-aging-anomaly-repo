# Richard F. Gordon Jr. - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Richard Francis Gordon Jr.

**Birth:** October 5, 1929, Seattle, Washington

**Death:** November 6, 2017 (age 88), San Marcos, California

**Cause of Death:** Not specified on Wikipedia

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Final Rank:** Captain (O-6)
- **Service Period:** 1951-1972

---

## Education
- **BS in Chemistry:** University of Washington (1951)
- Naval Aviator wings (1953)
- U.S. Naval Test Pilot School (1957)
- Naval Postgraduate School attendance

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Test pilot and naval aviator (1953-1963)
- First project test pilot for F4H-1 Phantom II
- Won Bendix Trophy race LA to NYC (May 1961) at 869.74 mph
- 4,500+ flight hours (3,500 in jets)

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 1963)

**Missions:**
1. **Gemini 11** (1966) — Pilot; 2 EVAs totaling 2 hours 41 minutes
2. **Apollo 12** (1969) — Command Module Pilot; orbited Moon 45 times while crewmates landed

**Total Time in Space:** 315 hours 53 minutes
**EVA time:** 2 hours 41 minutes
**Post-flight:** Chief of advanced programs (1971); Space Shuttle design contributions
**Retirement:** January 1, 1972

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Executive Vice President, New Orleans Saints (1972-1976)
- General Manager, Energy Developers Limited (1977)
- President, Resolution Engineering and Development Company (1978)
- President, Astro Sciences Corporation (1982+)
- Technical advisor for CBS miniseries "Space" (1984)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- Wrote foreword for Al Worden's *Falling to Earth* (2011, age 82)
- Contributed foreword to *Footprints in the Dust* (2010, age 81)
- Hobbies included water skiing and golf
- Died at 88; no documented health limitations mentioned
