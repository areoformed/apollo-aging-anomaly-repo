# Roger B. Chaffee - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** Roger Bruce Chaffee

**Birth:** February 15, 1935, Grand Rapids, Michigan

**Death:** January 27, 1967 (age 31), Cape Kennedy, Florida

**Cause of Death:** Asphyxia from toxic gases during Apollo 1 cabin fire; cardiac arrest from oxygen deprivation with burns as contributing factors

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** U.S. Navy
- **Rank:** Lieutenant Commander (promoted February 1, 1966)
- Commissioned as ensign August 22, 1957
- Over 2,300 flight hours (2,000+ in jets)

---

## Education
- Illinois Institute of Technology (1953-1954)
- **BS in Aeronautical Engineering:** Purdue University (1957)
- **MS in Reliability Engineering:** Air Force Institute of Technology (1963)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Naval aviator with Heavy Photographic Squadron 62; flew 82 reconnaissance missions over Cuba (April 1960-October 1962) during Cuban Missile Crisis; earned Air Medal

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (October 18, 1963)
**Roles:** CAPCOM for Gemini 3 and 4

**Mission:** Apollo 1 — assigned as pilot; killed in cabin fire during pre-launch test January 27, 1967

---

## Post-NASA Career
Not applicable — died at age 31.
