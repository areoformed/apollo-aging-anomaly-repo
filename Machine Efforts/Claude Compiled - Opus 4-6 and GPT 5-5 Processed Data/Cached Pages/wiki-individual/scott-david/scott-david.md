# David Scott - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** David Randolph Scott

**Date of Birth:** June 6, 1932

**Date of Death:** Not deceased (age 93 as of 2026)

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Air Force
- **Rank:** Brigadier General (honorary promotion August 23, 2024; retired as Colonel in 1975)
- Fighter pilot in Europe (1956-1960)
- 5,600+ logged flying hours

---

## Education
- **BS (Military Science):** United States Military Academy, West Point (1954, 5th in class of 633)
- **MS in Aeronautics/Astronautics:** MIT (1962)
- **Engineer in Aeronautics/Astronautics:** MIT (1962)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Fighter pilot, 32nd Tactical Fighter Squadron, Netherlands (1956-1960)
- USAF Experimental Flight Test Pilot School, Edwards AFB (Class 62C)
- Aerospace Research Pilot School (Class IV)

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** NASA Astronaut Group 3 (1963)

**Missions:**
1. **Gemini 8** (March 16, 1966) — Pilot; emergency abort after docking malfunction
2. **Apollo 9** (March 3-13, 1969) — Command Module Pilot; 10 days in orbit
3. **Apollo 15** (July 26-August 7, 1971) — Commander; fourth lunar landing; drove Lunar Roving Vehicle; performed hammer-and-feather experiment

**Total Time in Space:** 22 days, 18 hours, 54 minutes
**EVAs:** 5 total; 20 hours 46 minutes
**Seventh person to walk on the Moon**

---

## Post-NASA Career
- Deputy Director, then Center Director of Dryden Flight Research Center (1975-1977)
- Founded Scott Science and Technology, Inc.
- Film consultant: *Apollo 13*, *From the Earth to the Moon*, *In the Shadow of the Moon*, others
- Co-authored *Two Sides of the Moon* with cosmonaut Alexei Leonov (2006)
- Worked on Chandrayaan-1 lunar orbiter research (Brown University)

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- **Still living at age 93 as of 2026**
- Active consultant on space projects through 2000s
- Co-authored book with Leonov (2006, age 74)
- Pursued lawsuit against Bulova over commercial rights (2017-2018, ages 85-86)
- Received honorary Brigadier General promotion (August 2024, age 92)
- Living in Los Angeles; described as collaborating on visualization projects for lunar/Mars exploration
- Only living commander of a lunar-landing spacecraft
