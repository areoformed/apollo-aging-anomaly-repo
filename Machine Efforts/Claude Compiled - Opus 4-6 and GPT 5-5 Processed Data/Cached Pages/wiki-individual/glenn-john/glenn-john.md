# John Glenn: Biographical Data

**Full Name:** John Herschel Glenn Jr.

**Date of Birth:** July 18, 1921 (Cambridge, Ohio)

**Date of Death:** December 8, 2016 (Columbus, Ohio, age 95)

**Cause of Death:** Not specified in Wikipedia extract

---

## Military Service

**Branches:** U.S. Navy (aviation cadet), U.S. Marine Corps

**Final Rank:** Colonel

**Years of Service:** 1941-1965

**Combat Experience:**
- World War II: 57 combat missions in Marshall Islands; 2 Distinguished Flying Crosses, 10 Air Medals
- Korean War: 63 missions with F9F Panther; 27 missions with F-86 Sabre; shot down 3 MiG-15s (final air victories of war); 2 additional Distinguished Flying Crosses, 8 Air Medals
- Test Pilot: Conducted first supersonic transcontinental flight (July 16, 1957); 5th Distinguished Flying Cross for this mission

---

## Education

**Bachelor of Science** from Muskingum University (incomplete formal degree; met requirements through additional coursework)

**Field:** Chemistry (studies); Physics course credit via Civilian Pilot Training Program (1941)

---

## Pre-NASA Career

- **Test Pilot (1954-1959):** U.S. Naval Test Pilot School graduate; tested FJ-3 Fury, F7U Cutlass, F8U Crusader
- **Bureau of Aeronautics (1956-1959):** Fighter Design Branch, Navy; attended University of Maryland

---

## NASA Career

**Selection:** Mercury Seven astronauts (announced April 9, 1959)

**Missions:**
1. **Mercury-Atlas 6** ("Friendship 7," February 20, 1962): First American to orbit Earth; 3 orbits; 4 hours 55 minutes flight time
2. **STS-95** (October-November 1998): Payload specialist; 9 days 19 hours 54 minutes; age 77

**Honors:**
- NASA Distinguished Service Medal (1962)
- Congressional Space Medal of Honor (1978)
- U.S. Astronaut Hall of Fame (1990)
- Presidential Medal of Freedom (2012)

---

## Post-NASA Career

**Business (1965-1974):**
- RC Cola: Vice President of Corporate Development, Board Director; later President of Royal Crown International
- Holiday Inn partnerships: Co-owned four hotels near Disney World (starting 1973)

**Political Career (1974-1999):**
- **U.S. Senator from Ohio:** December 24, 1974 - January 3, 1999 (24 years)
- **Senate Committee on Governmental Affairs Chair:** 1987-1995
- **1984 Presidential Campaign:** Announced candidacy April 21, 1983; withdrew March 16, 1984
- Senate accomplishments: Nuclear Non-Proliferation Act of 1978 (chief author); nuclear weapons facility safety investigations; "Keating Five" scandal (reprimanded for "poor judgment")

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+

**Space Flight at Age 77:**
- Flew STS-95 mission in October-November 1998, becoming "the oldest person to enter Earth orbit" and the only person to fly in both Mercury and Space Shuttle programs

**Professional Activities (Post-1999):**
- Remained a public figure through death at age 95 in 2016
- Received Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2012 (age 91)
