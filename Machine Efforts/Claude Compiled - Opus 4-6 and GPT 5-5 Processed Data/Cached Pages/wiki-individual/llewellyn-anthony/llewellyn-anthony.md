# John Anthony Llewellyn - Biographical Data

**Full Name:** John Anthony Llewellyn

**Date of Birth:** April 22, 1933, Cardiff, Wales, UK

**Date of Death:** July 2, 2013 (aged 80), Madeira Beach, Florida, USA

**Cause of Death:** Stroke

---

## Military Service
No military service documented

---

## Education
- **B.Sc.** (1955): Chemistry, University College, Cardiff
- **Ph.D.** (1958): Chemistry, Cardiff University

---

## Pre-NASA Career
- Post-doctoral fellow, National Research Council of Canada, Ottawa (1958-1960)
- Research Associate, Florida State University Chemistry Department (1960)
- Assistant Professor, Florida State University (1960-1964)
- Associate Professor, School of Engineering Science and Department of Chemistry, Florida State University (1964+)
- Diving instructor; certified by Jacques Cousteau

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected as scientist-astronaut, NASA Astronaut Group 6 (August 1967)
- One of two non-US citizens selected
- Participated in flight training
- Dropped out of flight school and resigned from NASA in September 1968 (unable to fly jets with blacked-out cockpit)

---

## Missions Flown
None

---

## Time in Space
Zero

---

## Post-NASA Career
- NOAA aquanaut, Hydrolab crew (1971-1976, approximately 5 years)
- Full Professor, Department of Chemical Engineering, University of South Florida (1971+)
- Director of College of Engineering computing department
- University Director of Academic Computing
- Professor Emeritus until death
- Research focus: drug and gene delivery methods

---

## Evidence of Vitality at Age 80+
- Presented at the 2010 American Society for Gene and Cell Therapy (age 77)
- Session leader at 2010 Gordon Conference in Bioelectrochemistry (age 77)
- Active academic until stroke in 2013 (age 80)
- Died shortly after turning 80; was professionally active up to that point
