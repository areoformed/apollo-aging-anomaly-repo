**Full Name:** Gerald Paul Carr

**Date of Birth:** August 22, 1932, Denver, Colorado

**Date of Death:** August 26, 2020 (age 88), Albany, New York

**Cause of Death:** Not specified in article

---

## Military Service
- **Branch:** United States Marine Corps
- **Rank:** Colonel (retired September 1975)
- **Flight experience:** Over 8,000 flying hours (5,365 jet hours)
- **Aircraft:** F9F Cougar, F-6A Skyray, F-8 Crusader, F-4, T-38, among others

---

## Education
- B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, University of Southern California (1954)
- B.S. in Aeronautical Engineering, Naval Postgraduate School (1961)
- M.S. in Aeronautical Engineering, Princeton University (1962)

---

## Pre-NASA Career
Fighter pilot; postgraduate training in aeronautics

---

## NASA Career Highlights
- Selected April 1966, NASA Astronaut Group 5
- CAPCOM for Apollo 8 and Apollo 12
- Commander, Skylab 4 (November 16, 1973 – February 8, 1974)

---

## Missions Flown
- **Skylab 4** (November 16, 1973 – February 8, 1974) — Commander

## Time in Space
84 days, 1 hour, 15 minutes; 15 hours 51 minutes in three EVAs

---

## Post-NASA Career (retired June 1977)
- Manager, Bovay Engineers, Inc. (Houston, Texas)
- Senior consultant, Applied Research, Inc. (1981–1983)
- Manager, University of Texas 300-inch Telescope Project (1983–1985)
- Founded CAMUS, Inc. (1984); provided technical support for International Space Station design
