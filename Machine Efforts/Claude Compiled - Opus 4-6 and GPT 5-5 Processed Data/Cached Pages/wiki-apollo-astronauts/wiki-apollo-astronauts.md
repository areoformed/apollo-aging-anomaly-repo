# Apollo Mission Crew Rosters (Apollo 7-17) + Apollo Applications
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Apollo_astronauts
Cached: 2026-05-18

## Apollo 7 (October 1968) — Earth Orbit
- Commander: Wally Schirra
- CMP: Donn Eisele
- LMP: Walter Cunningham

## Apollo 8 (December 1968) — Lunar Orbit (no landing)
- Commander: Frank Borman
- CMP: Jim Lovell
- LMP: Bill Anders

## Apollo 9 (March 1969) — Earth Orbit (LM test)
- Commander: James McDivitt
- CMP: David Scott
- LMP: Rusty Schweickart

## Apollo 10 (May 1969) — Lunar Orbit (LM test, no landing)
- Commander: Tom Stafford
- CMP: John Young
- LMP: Gene Cernan

## Apollo 11 (July 1969) — Lunar Landing
- Commander: Neil Armstrong (WALKED)
- CMP: Michael Collins
- LMP: Buzz Aldrin (WALKED)

## Apollo 12 (November 1969) — Lunar Landing
- Commander: Pete Conrad (WALKED)
- CMP: Dick Gordon
- LMP: Alan Bean (WALKED)

## Apollo 13 (April 1970) — Aborted (circumlunar)
- Commander: Jim Lovell
- CMP: Jack Swigert
- LMP: Fred Haise

## Apollo 14 (February 1971) — Lunar Landing
- Commander: Alan Shepard (WALKED)
- CMP: Stuart Roosa
- LMP: Edgar Mitchell (WALKED)

## Apollo 15 (July 1971) — Lunar Landing
- Commander: David Scott (WALKED)
- CMP: Alfred Worden
- LMP: James Irwin (WALKED)

## Apollo 16 (April 1972) — Lunar Landing
- Commander: John Young (WALKED)
- CMP: Ken Mattingly
- LMP: Charles Duke (WALKED)

## Apollo 17 (December 1972) — Lunar Landing
- Commander: Gene Cernan (WALKED)
- CMP: Ronald Evans
- LMP: Harrison Schmitt (WALKED)
