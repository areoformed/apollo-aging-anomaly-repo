# NASA Astronaut Group 7 — MOL Transfer
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA_Astronaut_Group_7
Cached: 2026-05-18

Selection Date: August 14, 1969
Transferred from USAF Manned Orbiting Laboratory program.
Slayton accepted those 35 or younger (7 of 14 remaining MOL personnel).
All seven eventually flew Space Shuttle missions.

1. Karol J. Bobko
2. Robert L. Crippen
3. C. Gordon Fullerton
4. Henry W. Hartsfield Jr.
5. Robert F. Overmyer
6. Donald H. Peterson
7. Richard H. Truly
